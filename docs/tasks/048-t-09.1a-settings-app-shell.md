## Goal

The Settings window shell: sidebar, local search, chrome, traffic lights, and accessibility.

## Scope

- Keyboard navigation and AT-SPI roles; design-system components only.

## Explicitly deferred

## Reference UI/UX (macOS screenshots)

Near-verbatim target: the macOS System Settings shell in the local captures
(`docs/reference/macos/`; never shipped, see
[14-risks.md](../design/14-risks.md)), distilled in
[System_Preferences.md](../reference/System_Preferences.md).

Sections and rows (observed order):

- **Window**: rounded with a drop shadow; translucent sidebar, opaque detail
  pane; follows the desktop light/dark appearance. Chrome: traffic lights;
  back/forward chevron pills (forward dimmed at the end of history) and the
  current pane's title.
- **Sidebar**: search field at top (`Search` placeholder, magnifier glyph);
  then the account row; then one continuous, scrollable list of icon + label
  pane rows in reference order: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Network, Battery, General,
  Accessibility, Appearance, Apple Intelligence & Siri, Desktop & Dock,
  Displays, Menu Bar, Spotlight, Wallpaper, Notifications, Sound, Focus,
  Screen Time, Lock Screen, Privacy & Security, Biometrics & Password,
  Users & Groups, Internet Accounts, Keyboard, Mouse, Trackpad, Printers &
  Scanners. The selected pane carries a tinted rounded highlight with
  accent-colored text; rows appear as their panes ship (no half-panes).
- **Header card**: large pane icon, pane title, one-line description.
- **Grouped inset sections**: icon + label rows; trailing chevron when the
  row pushes a deeper screen; toggles and pickers inline on their row.

Adapt or omit (Apple-only): the Apple Account row (our **Users & Groups**
pane, T-15.11, covers accounts); `Apple Intelligence & Siri` and Screen Time
(macOS-specific, not planned); Spotlight (our Search pane, deferred);
AppleCare & Warranty and AirDrop & Continuity rows; Internet Accounts
(deferred, not core).

Before reporting done, run the app and **look at it**:

- Launch the nested session (`make demo`), open the surface this task changed,
  and capture it (e.g. `spectacle -b -n -f -o /tmp/<task>.png`).

  compositor window — layout, materials, text, and no stray artifacts.
