## Goal

The Dock reads as a macOS Tahoe floating glass dock: a continuous translucent
plate with a bright rim and soft shadow, rounded-square icon tiles at a
consistent inset, and coherent hover/press/running states — reproduced with our
own geometry, tokens, and material approximation, never Apple assets.

## Context (read first)

- `shell/dock/Dock.qml` — `dockBar` plate (`~1414-1426`), hover
  (`~1671-1705`), constants (`~169-202`).
- `shell/dock/DockEntry.qml` — hover highlight, pressed scale, lifted shadow,
  focus ring, indicator, badges (`~173-326`).
- `shell/dock/DockGlyph.qml` — placeholder tile radius (`~60-75`), themed

- `compositor/src/window/backdrop.rs` — `MaterialRole`, `BackdropSpec`,
  `backdrop_layers`, degrade tiers; the chrome backdrop is a flat-tone
  approximation (ADR [0013](../design/adr/0013-backdrop-blur-pass.md), ADR
  [0015](../design/adr/0015-material-degrade-tiers.md)).
- `design-system/tokens/tokens.json` — `controls.dock` (spacing/size) and
  `material.chromeOpacity`/`chromeBlur`; `scripts/gen-tokens.py` regenerates
  `Theme.qml` (do not edit generated files).
- Reference captures: macOS Tahoe Dock under `docs/reference/macos/`
  (gitignored, never shipped); measure before choosing numbers.
- ADRs [0089](../design/adr/0089-dock-plate-geometry-and-live-panel-rect.md)

  [0091](../design/adr/0091-dock-tahoe-floating-glass-language.md) (language).

## Current behavior (investigated, T101 era)

- The plate is a single flat `Rectangle` (chrome color at `chromeOpacity`) with
  a 1 px border and no rim/highlight/shadow — it reads as a flat white/grey
  slab, not glass.
- Themed icons fill the entire tile with `PreserveAspectFit` and no squircle
  mask/inset; the placeholder tile's radius (`0.24 * size`) is unrelated to the
  plate and inconsistent with Tahoe proportions.
- Hover is a flat `controlFill` rectangle at 0.6 opacity; the divider and
  running indicator are plain.
- The compositor's chrome backdrop uses one `MaterialRole::Chrome` spec for the
  menu bar and the Dock, so the Dock cannot be tuned independently.

## Explicitly deferred

- True liquid-glass refraction (a real texture sampler); the flat layered
  approximation is the shipping form and is labeled as such.
- Fan/grid stack layouts → T-14.7h/T-14.7k.
- Tahoe styling for the menu bar and other chrome (a later material sweep).
- Icon artwork itself (we render the user's theme icons; no Apple assets).

## Design notes

- Reproduce the language, never Apple's bitmaps (`14-risks.md`); keep all
  values in tokens and all geometry original.
- The plate remains cosmetic (ADR 0089): the compositor's reserved zone and
  the window layout do not change with the material.
- Degrade must be intentional: `Minimal` is a clean opaque capsule, not a
  broken glass.
- The Dock is not a gallery component; visual verification is pixel tests plus
  committed captures, with the human track sign-off comparing to the reference.
