## Goal

The Dock reads as a macOS Tahoe floating glass dock: a continuous translucent
plate with a bright rim and soft shadow, rounded-square icon tiles at a
consistent inset, and coherent hover/press/running states — reproduced with our
own geometry, tokens, and material approximation, never Apple assets.

## Context (read first)

- `shell/dock/Dock.qml` — `dockBar` plate (`~1414-1426`), hover

- `shell/dock/DockEntry.qml` — hover highlight, pressed scale, lifted shadow,

## Current behavior (investigated, T101 era)

- The plate is a single flat `Rectangle` (chrome color at `chromeOpacity`) with
  a 1 px border and no rim/highlight/shadow — it reads as a flat white/grey
  slab, not glass.
- Themed icons fill the entire tile with `PreserveAspectFit` and no squircle
  mask/inset; the placeholder tile's radius (`0.24 * size`) is unrelated to the
  plate and inconsistent with Tahoe proportions.
- Hover is a flat `controlFill` rectangle at 0.6 opacity; the divider and
  running indicator are plain.
- The compositor's chrome backdrop uses one `MaterialRole::Chrome` spec for the
  menu bar and the Dock, so the Dock cannot be tuned independently.

## Explicitly deferred

- True liquid-glass refraction (a real texture sampler); the flat layered
  approximation is the shipping form and is labeled as such.
- Fan/grid stack layouts → T-14.7h/T-14.7k.
- Tahoe styling for the menu bar and other chrome (a later material sweep).
- Icon artwork itself (we render the user's theme icons; no Apple assets).

## Design notes

- Reproduce the language, never Apple's bitmaps (`14-risks.md`); keep all
  values in tokens and all geometry original.
- The plate remains cosmetic (ADR 0089): the compositor's reserved zone and
  the window layout do not change with the material.
- Degrade must be intentional: `Minimal` is a clean opaque capsule, not a
  broken glass.
- The Dock is not a gallery component; visual verification is pixel tests plus
  committed captures, with the human track sign-off comparing to the reference.

## Hand-off

**Landed.** The Dock is a layered floating glass plate (translucent fill, bright
interior rim, hairline border, soft shadow) over the compositor's live backdrop,
with token squircles and shared hover/press/running states. ADR
[0102](../design/adr/0102-dock-material-role-and-qml-glass-layers.md).

**Reference measurements.** The only local dock reference is
`docs/reference/macos/Screenshot 2026-09-18 at 9.20.18 PM.png` (a classic
translucent rounded dock, not the Tahoe build), with the vision sweep at
`docs/reference/macos/notes/`. It reads as: a floating plate with a visible
screen-edge gap (~10 px), plate height ~1/18 of the output, corner radius ~1/4
to 1/3 of plate height, icons ~80–90 % of the plate height at ~20–25 px end
padding, a soft top-edge highlight and shadow but no hard border, a centred
running underline, and a 1–2 px divider before Trash. Those ratios anchored the
chosen values: `dock.radius = primitive.radius.xl (20)` on a ~68 px bar
(≈0.29), rim/border/plate opacities 0.7/0.55/0.42, shadow blur 20/offset 6,
icon radius ratio 0.24 with a 0.06 inset. The Tahoe-specific layer names are
ours (original geometry only); no Apple bitmap is used.

**Final token names.** Semantic colors `dockFill`, `dockRim`, `dockBorder`,
`dockShadow`, `dockHoverFill`, `dockDivider`, `dockIndicator`; semantic material
`dockOpacity`/`dockBlur` (light 0.5/30, dark 0.42/34); `component.dock.radius =
xl`; nested `component.dock.plate` (fill/rim/border/shadow), `.icon`
(radiusRatio/inset), `.hover` (fillOpacity/radiusRatio), `.indicator` (opacity),
`.divider` (opacity/width/heightRatio). Regenerated `Theme.qml` and
`design_tokens.rs`.

**Material-role choice.** New `MaterialRole::Dock`, selected by the
`df_layer_surface` namespace `"dock"` on persistent surfaces
(`from_layer_namespace`); the menu bar stays `Chrome`, overlays stay `Popup`.
`ChromeSurface` now carries `namespace`. The one-backdrop-pass invariant and the
degrade tiers are unchanged; `Minimal` drops the frost and the plate reads as a
clean capsule.

**Deviation (documented).** Arbitrary square theme artwork is inset and fitted,
not per-pixel squircle-masked: the headless software scene graph has no mask
pass, so a real mask would be GPU-only and invisible to the pixel tests. True
squircle masking is deferred with liquid-glass refraction (ADR 0102). Also no
degrade-tier signal reaches QML, so the QML rim/border always draw; only the
compositor frost degrades.

**Verification.** `ctest --test-dir build --output-on-failure` 53/53 (tst_dock
175, incl. five new T-14.7j cases); `cargo test --workspace` and `make e2e`
green; fmt/clippy/gen-tokens/check-design-tokens/check-no-capture-grab/gallery
strict green. Live: `make dock-tahoe-capture` produced
`docs/captures/t14-dock-tahoe-{light,dark,reduced}.png`; vision inspection
confirms the plate reads as translucent glass with a bright top rim and soft
shadow (never a flat slab), rounded-square tiles at a consistent inset, visible
hover/magnify/press states, and a clean capsule at the `Minimal` tier with no
clipping. `check-desktop-names.sh` still fails only on the pre-existing
StatusNotifier/zoo lines.

**What T-14.7k must know.** Read `plateRect` for geometry and
`dockPlate.plateX/plateY` for child placement; the plate group is clipped and
per-position offset. The rim is orientation-aware (interior edge). Reuse
`Theme.controls.dock.icon.radiusRatio`/`.inset` and `tileRadius`/`hoverRadius`
for any new pinned-folder entry; keep the Trash its own bin. A new pinned-folder
glyph must not assume a top rim or a QML degrade switch. See the PROGRESS
section for the full list.
