## Hand-off

**Status: done (attempt 1).** The Settings Battery pane and the Control Center
Battery tile ship as one functional unit over the T-15.6a adapter, and the
bridge host's `Battery` interface gains its one write. See the T122 section in
`docs/PROGRESS.md` and ADR [0129](../design/adr/0129-battery-pane-and-tile.md).

What landed:

- `services/system-status`: `battery_view` carries health/capacity/cycles,
  charge state, and the `profiles` list + active profile; `StatusHost::
  set_active_profile` and `BatteryInterface::SetActiveProfile` are the write.
- `apps/settings`: `BatteryClient` seam, `Settings` singleton `battery`/
  `batteryAvailable`/`refreshBattery`/`setPowerProfile`, and `BatteryPane.qml`
  (Power Mode picker, Battery Health/Charging info rows, Usage History group).
- `shell`: the Control Center gets a Battery tile (index 9) reading the same
  host view; `onBatterySettingsRequested`; panel height 980 → 1040.

Deviations from the T-15.6a hand-off / plan (all recorded in ADR 0129):

- **No settingsd keys were added.** The active profile is daemon state, not a
  preference; the pane writes it through the host, unlike Mission Control's
  settingsd-key path.
- **Usage History is an honest absent state.** UPower exposes no charge history,
  so the `Last 24 Hours`/`Last 10 Days` switch drives the empty-state caption
  and the two chart frames show axis labels + a "no history" caption. A history
  provider remains T-15.x. `Options…`/`?` are omitted rather than shipped dead.
- **Low Power Mode maps to the three ppd profiles** (`power-saver`/`balanced`/
  `performance`); ppd has no `Never`/`Always`. Apple-only `i` panels are
  replaced by UPower capacity/cycles and the charge estimate.
- The Control Center panel is now **360x1040**; the T-15.5b capture script's
  crop was updated.

Tests (all green): `cargo test -p dragonfruit-system-status`,
`ctest --test-dir build --output-on-failure -j4` (60/60), `make e2e` (EXIT 0),
`cargo fmt --all -- --check`, clippy, `make check-design-tokens check-tokens
check-no-capture-grab`, `./scripts/check-gallery-snapshots.py`.

Live visual check: `bash scripts/capture-t15-battery-pane.sh` produced
`docs/captures/t15-6b-battery-pane.png` (2088x1410) and
`docs/captures/t15-6b-battery-control-center.png` (360x1040). Vision confirmed

`Normal`/`On Battery`, the absent-state chart caption, and the Battery tile
(`82% · Balanced`, balance-scale glyph, `Battery Settings…`) above an unclipped
Clipboard tile — no blank areas or clipping.
