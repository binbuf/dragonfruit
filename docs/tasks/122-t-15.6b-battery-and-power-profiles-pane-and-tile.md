## Reference UI/UX (macOS screenshots)

Local art direction only — images in `docs/reference/macos/` (never shipped;
see [14-risks.md](../design/14-risks.md)), distilled in
[System_Preferences.md](../reference/System_Preferences.md). Target: mirror
the macOS Battery pane (`Screenshot 2026-09-18 at 9.21.05 PM.png`) nearly
verbatim in style, information architecture, section order, row labels,
control types, and wording.

Sections and rows (observed order):

- **Low Power Mode** — popup showing `Never`; maps to the
  power-profiles-daemon profile picker (T-15.6a).
- **Battery Health** — info row with right-aligned value (`Normal`) and an
  `i` info button; provider TBD (T-15.x).
- **Charging** — info row with an `i` info button; provider TBD (T-15.x).
- **Last 24 Hours / Last 10 Days** — segmented range switch for the charts
  below.
- **Battery Level** — bar chart over the selected range; maps to UPower
  charge history when the daemon keeps it, else provider TBD (T-15.x).
- **Last charged to 100%** — row with timestamp secondary text; provider
  TBD (T-15.x).
- **Screen On Usage** — line chart; provider TBD (T-15.x).
- **Options...** — footer button (with a `?` help button beside it);
  provider TBD (T-15.x).

Adapt or omit (Apple-only):

- The `i` panels behind `Battery Health` and `Charging` cover Apple-only
  hardware health and charging optimizations (optimized charging, charge
  limit); keep the generic rows above, omit the Apple-only detail panes.
- Keep near-verbatim: the power-mode picker, range switch, battery-level
  chart, and timestamp row are generic enough to keep.
