## Goal

Stabilize the scene/panel element IDs and damage so a stationary Dock with an unchanged wallpaper does not damage the full output every frame.

## Context (read first)

- Parent spec: `docs/tasks/0.1.1/reference/190-t-21.3-panel-region-material-and-scanout.md`.
- T190b region-sized render and caches.
- `SceneTextureElement`, `blur_panel_texture()`, and the scene/panel cache lifetimes.

## Scope

- [ ] Give the scene and each panel a stable `Id` per output/panel slot (or cache the element instances) and keep a stable `current_commit` where appropriate.
- [ ] Ensure a stationary Dock with an unchanged wallpaper produces no full-output damage; add damage unit tests.
- [ ] Check `MultiFrame` synchronization and `GlesTexture` lifetimes under panel add/remove; commit a nested capture and the damage test.

## Out of scope

- Region-sized render/caching (T190b).
- Direct scanout policy and counters (T190d).
- New material design.
- Refresh scheduling.

## Design notes

- Keep the full-output path available as a diagnostic/rollback switch.
- Do not change the visual result at the panel.
- No per-frame allocation or full-output damage for a settled scene.
