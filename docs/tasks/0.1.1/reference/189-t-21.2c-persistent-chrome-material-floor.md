## Goal

Make the degrade ladder treat the Dock and menu bar as **persistent chrome**,
not as transient overlays. Under budget pressure they stay at `Reduced` (blur
on) at worst on the GPU path; if even `Reduced` cannot meet the deadline, they
use the deliberate milky fallback rather than silently becoming a flat
translucent slab. The 12/180 hysteresis becomes refresh-aware so it does not
flap or recover too quickly at 120/144 Hz.

  Drawer}`; `Chrome` and `Dock` are persistent, `Popup`/`Drawer` are
  transient.
- `compositor/src/window/degrade.rs` — `DegradeTier::{Full, Reduced, Minimal}`,

## Scope

- [ ] Add a persistent/transient classification to `MaterialRole`
  (`Chrome`/`Dock` persistent; `Popup`/`Drawer` transient).
- [ ] Make the effective material **per role** (and per output where the
  backend can differ):
  - persistent roles never select below `Reduced` while the sampled path is
    available,
  - transient roles may reach `Minimal`,
  - if the GPU cannot meet the deadline at `Reduced`, persistent roles use the
    milky fallback (`blur_active=0`) with an explicit fallback reason, not a
    flat translucent fill,
  - software/headless remains `Minimal` with the deterministic milky plate.
- [ ] Extend `df_output.material_state` additively (v4) with a role/namespace
  field, or add a second event that carries the same state keyed by role.
  Keep change-only emission and the existing fields' meaning; update the
  protocol conformance test.
- [ ] Update the shell to apply the effective state per role. The Dock/menu bar
  and every popup-family surface must read the same state; do not duplicate
  the degrade policy in QML.
- [ ] Make hysteresis refresh-aware:
  - replace fixed frame counts with time windows (or scale them by the current
    refresh period) so a 144 Hz panel does not downgrade/recover ~2.4× faster
    than a 60 Hz panel,
  - keep the 12-frame/180-frame behavior at 60 Hz as the reference,
  - prevent Full↔Reduced oscillation during a steady pointer sweep.
- [ ] Cover fallback roles: Dock, menu bar, Dock popovers, menu popovers,
  Control Center, notifications, OSD, and Applications drawer. A surface that
  assumes blur must have a deliberate milky fallback.
- [ ] Add forced-tier/load tests that pin `Full`, `Reduced`, and `Minimal` and
  verify the effective per-role state and the shell's applied properties.

## Out of scope

- Material panel-region cost (→ T190).
- Multi-GPU/rotated material (existing fallback; T193 records it).
- New visual design or token values; this is policy and fallback coverage.

## Design notes

- The floor is **perceptual**, not a mandatory GPU-blur floor: a convincing
  milky plate is acceptable if the GPU cannot sustain `Reduced`; a flat
  translucent plate is not.
- The Dock/menu bar must not silently lose their material because a transient
  overlay (Mission Control, drawer, OSD) pushed the global tier down. That is
  the exact failure this task exists to prevent.
- `material_state` is a state broadcast, not a per-frame event. Emit only on
  effective change; keep it additive and versioned.
- The shell's fallback tokens remain the single visual source. Do not hardcode
  a second neutral in C++ or QML.
- Hysteresis should be expressed in time or in refresh-scaled frames, and the
  policy must be pure/testable without a compositor.
- A forced tier is a test/diagnostic path; it must still respect the
  persistent-chrome floor or explicitly report that the floor is overridden.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Under a forced/steady over-budget load, the Dock and menu bar stay at
  `Reduced` or better (or report the explicit milky fallback); they do not
  drop to `Minimal` while the sampled path is available.
- [ ] Transient overlays may reach `Minimal` without changing the persistent
  chrome's effective state.
- [ ] The shell receives per-role state and applies the correct fallback; a
  popup/OSD/Control Center surface no longer becomes a flat translucent slab
  when the sampled pass is absent.
- [ ] A 60-second Dock sweep at 60/120/144 Hz shows no repeated Full↔Reduced
  flapping; `degrade stats` records bounded downgrades/upgrades.
- [ ] Forced `Minimal` still renders the token-driven milky plate; the Dock and
  menu bar remain deliberate in light and dark.
- [ ] Protocol conformance observes one `material_state` event per effective
  change, not per frame.
