## Goal

Render the scene into a region-sized or damaged cached texture and reallocate only on region/mode/scale change. Stable IDs and damage are T190c.

## Context (read first)

- Parent spec: `docs/tasks/0.1.1/reference/190-t-21.3-panel-region-material-and-scanout.md`.
- T190a region helper.
- `blur_panel_texture()`, `SceneTextureElement`, and the scene/panel caches.

## Scope

- [ ] Render into a region-sized texture or update a cached full scene only in damaged regions; keep output-coordinate `src` mapping and translate the region origin correctly.
- [ ] Reallocate only on region size or output mode/scale change; one cache per output (and per GPU where required).
- [ ] Check `MultiFrame` synchronization and `GlesTexture` lifetimes under panel add/remove; commit a nested 1x/1.5x/2x capture and region/caching unit tests.

## Out of scope

- Stable IDs and damage (T190c).
- Direct scanout policy and counters (T190d).
- New material design.
- Refresh scheduling.

## Design notes

- Keep the full-output path available as a diagnostic/rollback switch.
- Do not change the visual result at the panel.
- No per-frame allocation.
