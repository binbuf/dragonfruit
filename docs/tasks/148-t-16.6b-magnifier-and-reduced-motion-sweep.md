## Hand-off

**State: done.** Two units landed, contract frozen in
[ADR 0155](../design/adr/0155-compositor-magnifier.md).

- **Compositor magnifier (per the `02-compositor.md` spec).**
  `compositor/src/magnifier.rs` is the pure policy — whole-output uniform
  zoom (1.0..=8.0), the scene point kept at the output centre,
  `follow-focus`/`follow-caret`, and both view↔scene mappings. The nested
  backend applies it as one `Rescale(+Relocate)` wrapper per element about the
  output centre; input maps the physical pointer/touch position back to the
  scene so hit-testing stays correct (relative deltas divided by the zoom). It
  is controlled and queried over the synthetic harness (`set magnifier`,
  `set magnifier-zoom`, `set magnifier-mode`, `set magnifier-center`,
  `query magnifier`). 10 model unit tests + `window_conformance.rs::
  magnifier_reports_its_view_transform_and_zoom`.
- **Reduced-motion sweep.** `compositor/tests/reduced_motion_sweep.rs` (part
  of `make e2e`): enumerates the generated Rust and QML motion catalogs
  (cross-checked; every token has an instant reduced variant), every QML
  animation site in `shell/`, `design-system/`, and `apps/` (each resolves
  through `Theme.motion` or gates on `Theme.reducedMotion`), and every
  compositor lifecycle kind (each routes through `Tween::from_motion`; the
  overview machine keeps its single-step branch). A new numeric-`duration:`
  animation fails the sweep.

**Deviations / follow-ups.**

- The magnifier's *durable trigger* is not wired: the Settings row, its
  `settingsd` key, and the private-protocol request that carries the value are
  outside this task's `compositor/`, `shell/` area. The magnifier is reachable
  through the synthetic seam today. This is the one remaining item.
- DRM magnification is not wired (nested is the demo/dev backend and what the
  capture proves); the DRM cursor must not scale with the scene.
- `follow-caret` is modelled and accepted but falls back to the focused window
  — the compositor has no caret-position source yet.

**Live visual check:** `docs/captures/t16-magnifier.png` (produced by
`scripts/capture-t16-magnifier.sh`) stacks the nested demo with the magnifier
off (top) and on at 2x centred (bottom). Vision confirmed the bottom is a
centred zoom of the top (UI enlarged, top bar and Dock cropped out) with no
blank/black/torn regions; toggling on→off returns to identical pixels.
`make e2e` EXIT 0 (`/tmp/opencode/t148-e2e-final.log`).

Hands off to T-16.7 (localization).
