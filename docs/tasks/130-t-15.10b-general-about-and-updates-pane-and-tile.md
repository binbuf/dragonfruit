**Sections and rows (observed order):** Observed in the Tahoe capture(s):
- Header card `General`: gray gear icon, title `General`, and the description
  "Manage your overall setup and preferences for Mac, such as software updates,
  device language, AirDrop, and more."
- Grouped inset disclosure rows (icon + label + trailing chevron, no secondary
  text), in observed order: `About`, `Software Update`, `Storage`, then the
  Apple-only `AppleCare & Warranty` and `AirDrop & Continuity`, then
  `AutoFill & Passwords`, `Date & Time`, `Language & Region`,
  `Login Items & Extensions`, `Sharing`, `Startup Disk`.
- `About` dialog (`About This Mac`): centered MacBook Pro illustration; heading
  `MacBook Pro`; sublabel `14-inch, M5`; static label/value info rows `Chip` =
  `Apple M5`, `Memory` = `16 GB`, `Serial number` = redacted in the capture
  (not readable), `macOS` = `Tahoe 26.6.2`; `More Info...` button.
- `About` footer: `Regulatory Certification` link and the Apple legal footer
  "™ and © 1983-2026 Apple Inc." / "All Rights Reserved."
- `Software Update`: maps to the distro update provider (T-15.10a) — check,
  install, reboot.
- Other rows keep the observed layout and labels; providers TBD (T-15.x).
- Header/chrome: centered pane title, back/forward chevron pill (forward dimmed), sidebar `Search` field, trailing `?` help.

**Apple-only (adapt or omit):** AppleCare & Warranty, AirDrop & Continuity,
and iCloud rows; sidebar `Apple Account`, `Apple Intelligence & Siri`, and
`Screen Time` rows; the About `Chip` value `Apple M5` and the Apple copyright
footer map to Dragonfruit equivalents. For About, the `macOS` value reads
`Tahoe 26.6.2` in the reference — Dragonfruit shows its own OS name/version.

## Hand-off

**Done.** The General pane and the Control Center Software Update tile ship as
one functional unit over the T-15.10a adapter, through the
`org.dragonfruit.SystemStatus1.Updates` bridge interface (ADR 0137). No new
settingsd keys: About is a live host read and the check/install/reboot writes
are explicit actions (the Storage precedent).

Landed:
- Rust host: `services/system-status/src/updates.rs` (`UpdatesHost` + view +
  report), `UPDATES_INTERFACE`, `UpdatesInterface`
  (`State`/`Refresh`/`Check`/`Install`/`Reboot`), `main.rs` wiring +
  `--print-updates`, `tests/updates.rs`; `UpdateProvider` gained a `Send` bound
  in the T-15.10a crate so the host can serve it from zbus threads.
- Settings: `apps/settings/UpdatesClient.{h,cpp}`, `SettingsBridge` properties +
  five invokables, `GeneralPane.qml` (About + Software Update dialogs),
  catalog/body/lint registration.
- Shell: `systemstatusclient`/`systemstatusmodel` updates plumbing,
  `shellcontroller` data + action/settings slots, 13th Control Center tile with
  a state-chosen action link.
- Design system: `general` and `software-update` painted glyphs.
- Docs: ADR 0137, `07-system-integration.md`, capture script + README.

Verification: `cargo test -p dragonfruit-system-status` (46+10+4+4+5),
`cargo test -p dragonfruit-update-adapter` (40+8), `ctest` 64/64, `make e2e`
EXIT 0, clippy/fmt clean, design-token/gallery checks clean. `make lint` still
fails only on the pre-existing `check-desktop-names` lines.

Deviations (recorded, not defects):
- `Storage` is omitted from the General row list — it is a first-class sidebar
  pane (T-15.2b). The other General rows have no provider yet and are omitted
  rather than shipped as dead controls. `More Info...`, the regulatory link, and
  the Apple copyright footer are omitted (nothing to open).
- The Control Center panel stayed a fixed 360×1160: the 13th tile fit only by
  tightening every tile's padding to `spacing.xs`. A 14th tile needs a scrolling
  panel or a taller nested output (not a bigger constant).

For the next task (T-15.11a Users & Groups): the bridge-host pattern here is
the template for another host-stack adapter; attach the concrete distro
`UpdateProvider` to `HostSystem` in `services/system-status/src/main.rs` when
packaging lands (T-16.9/T-16.10) — no pane/tile/adapter change is needed then.
Live visual check: `docs/captures/t15-10b-general-pane.png` and
`docs/captures/t15-10b-general-control-center.png` via
`bash scripts/capture-t15-general-pane.sh`; vision confirmed all rows/tiles with
no clipping or overlap.
