**Sections and rows (observed order):** Observed in the Tahoe capture(s):
- Header card `General`: gray gear icon, title `General`, and the description
  "Manage your overall setup and preferences for Mac, such as software updates,
  device language, AirDrop, and more."
- Grouped inset disclosure rows (icon + label + trailing chevron, no secondary
  text), in observed order: `About`, `Software Update`, `Storage`, then the
  Apple-only `AppleCare & Warranty` and `AirDrop & Continuity`, then
  `AutoFill & Passwords`, `Date & Time`, `Language & Region`,
  `Login Items & Extensions`, `Sharing`, `Startup Disk`.
- `About` dialog (`About This Mac`): centered MacBook Pro illustration; heading
  `MacBook Pro`; sublabel `14-inch, M5`; static label/value info rows `Chip` =
  `Apple M5`, `Memory` = `16 GB`, `Serial number` = redacted in the capture
  (not readable), `macOS` = `Tahoe 26.6.2`; `More Info...` button.
- `About` footer: `Regulatory Certification` link and the Apple legal footer
  "™ and © 1983-2026 Apple Inc." / "All Rights Reserved."
- `Software Update`: maps to the distro update provider (T-15.10a) — check,
  install, reboot.
- Other rows keep the observed layout and labels; providers TBD (T-15.x).
- Header/chrome: centered pane title, back/forward chevron pill (forward dimmed), sidebar `Search` field, trailing `?` help.

**Apple-only (adapt or omit):** AppleCare & Warranty, AirDrop & Continuity,
and iCloud rows; sidebar `Apple Account`, `Apple Intelligence & Siri`, and
`Screen Time` rows; the About `Chip` value `Apple M5` and the Apple copyright
footer map to Dragonfruit equivalents. For About, the `macOS` value reads
`Tahoe 26.6.2` in the reference — Dragonfruit shows its own OS name/version.
