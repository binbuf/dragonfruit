## Reference UI/UX (macOS screenshots)

Target the macOS General pane near-verbatim: local images in
`docs/reference/macos/` (never shipped; see
[14-risks.md](../design/14-risks.md)); distilled in
[System_Preferences.md](../reference/System_Preferences.md).

**Sections and rows (observed order):**

- **Header card**: gear icon, `General` title, and the one-line description
  ("Manage your overall setup and preferences for Mac, such as software
  updates, device language, AirDrop, and more."). Reuse the shell's header-card
  pattern from T-09.1a, minus the AirDrop clause.
- **Grouped inset rows**: icon + label + trailing chevron, no secondary text;
  every row pushes a deeper screen. Rows to build, in observed order
  (Apple-only rows omitted below): `About`, `Software Update`, `Storage`,
  `AutoFill & Passwords`, `Date & Time`, `Language & Region`,
  `Login Items & Extensions`, `Sharing`, `Startup Disk`.
- **`About`**: maps to the About screen reference — device illustration, device
  name, chip/processor, memory, serial number, and OS version with generic
  equivalents, plus a `More Info…` button.
- **`Software Update`**: maps to the distro update provider (T-15.10a):
  check, install, reboot.
- **Other rows**: keep the observed layout and labels now; providers TBD
  (T-15.x).

**Adapt or omit (Apple-only):** AppleCare & Warranty, AirDrop & Continuity,
and iCloud rows.
