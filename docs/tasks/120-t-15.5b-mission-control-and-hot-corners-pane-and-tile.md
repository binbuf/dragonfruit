## Goal

Settings pane and Control Center tile for Mission Control and hot corners as one functional unit.

## Scope

- Settings pane and Control Center tile shipped together (no half-panes).
- Absence case documented.

## Explicitly deferred

- None.

## Reference UI/UX (macOS screenshots)

Local macOS **Tahoe 26** captures only (`docs/reference/macos/` never ships; see
[14-risks.md](../design/14-risks.md)). The per-image inventory is
[`SystemSettings_DesktopDock.md`](../reference/macos/SystemSettings_DesktopDock.md);
the shared interface language and Linux adaptations are in
[ADR 0122](../design/adr/0122-tahoe-interface-language-across-chrome.md).
Distilled in [System_Preferences.md](../reference/System_Preferences.md).

**Mission Control and hot corners are not in the Tahoe capture set** (the pane
is listed under `Not captured`). The nearest captured reference is the
`Desktop & Stage Manager` section of `SystemSettings_DesktopDock.md`; its
observed rows are `Show items` (segmented control, `On Desktop` selected /
`In Stage Manager`), `Click wallpaper to show desktop` (popup, `Always`, with
the description "Click wallpaper to move windows out of the way, revealing
your desktop items and widgets."), `Stage Manager` (toggle, off, with the
description "Stage Manager arranges your recent windows into a single strip
for reduced clutter and quick access."), and `Show recent apps in
Stage Manager` (toggle, on). The Mission Control / hot corners pane follows
the Tahoe settings-window chrome from ADR 0122 — fixed sidebar with the
`Search` field, centered pane title, back/forward chevron pill, and grouped
inset content.

**Apple-only (adapt or omit):** `Stage Manager` and the `In Stage Manager`
segment map to our overview/workspaces; the Apple Account sidebar row is
omitted per ADR 0122.

## Live visual check (required)

Before reporting done, run the app and **look at it**:

- Launch the nested session (`make demo`), open the surface this task changed,
  and capture it (e.g. `spectacle -b -n -f -o /tmp/<task>.png`).

  compositor window — layout, materials, text, and no stray artifacts.

  render no pixels, so they miss exactly this class of bug. If the task has no
  surface of its own, still launch the demo and confirm the desktop renders.
- Note the capture path and your verdict in the Hand-off.
