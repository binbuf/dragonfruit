## Goal

Honor the decoration tier: SSD clients get our titlebar, CSD clients keep theirs, X11 Tier-2 gets ours, with no double decoration.

## Scope

- SSD client → our titlebar; CSD client → no titlebar; X11 Tier-2 → our titlebar.
- Verify X11 configure uses the titlebar insets through `configure_window_size`.
- Guard against double decoration in both directions.

## Explicitly deferred

- Fractional-scaled X11 (T-16).

## Acceptance (agent-completable)

- [ ] Qt (SSD), X11 (Tier-2), and CSD apps render correctly side by side.
- [ ] `xwayland_conformance` stays green.

## Test plan

- Headless X11 path via `x11rb`; nested three-client capture.
