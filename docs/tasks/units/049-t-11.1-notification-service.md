## Goal

Land a session D-Bus notification service and replace the Dock's badge-only failure path.

## Scope

- `org.freedesktop.Notifications` service: banners, history, actions, DND/Focus.
- Replace the Dock's transient launch-failure badge with a real notification.

## Explicitly deferred

- Rich media/grouping beyond MVP.
- Cross-device sync.

## Acceptance (agent-completable)

- [ ] A third-party `Notify` call produces a banner, a history entry, and an action round-trip.

## Test plan

- Integration: D-Bus `Notify`/`CloseNotification`/`GetCapabilities` with a test client.
- Unit: notification model.
