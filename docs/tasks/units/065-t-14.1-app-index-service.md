## Goal

Replace the interim resolver with a real identity/index service.

## Scope

- `org.dragonfruit.AppIndex1`: identity resolution (`app_id`/`WM_CLASS`/heuristics), themed icons.
- Install/uninstall/update events, launch registry (`app_running`/`app_exited`), recency, subscription.
- Retire the Dock's stub resolver; the miss set is the heuristic input.

## Explicitly deferred

- App Store/search integration (post-gate).

## Acceptance (agent-completable)

- [ ] Known apps resolve to the right identity/icon; events fire on install/uninstall.

## Test plan

- Unit: identity heuristics against the miss set; integration: D-Bus queries.
- Regression: Dock identity/grouping.

## Hand-off
