## Goal

Animate window close with the surface held as a ghost until completion, and prove interruptibility and clean teardown.

## Scope

- Fade/scale out; window input-inert and removed from the model only at completion.
- Interrupt/reverse/retarget without waiting for a transition to finish.
- Idle trace stays flat after the loop; no phantom focus target.

## Explicitly deferred

- Genie minimize (post-gate).

## Acceptance (agent-completable)

- [ ] Close resolves with no orphaned ghost and no phantom focus.
- [ ] `make e2e`/`make soak` green; capture (incl. reduced motion) committed.

## Test plan

- Headless: assert `Closed` after the animation and a flat idle trace after.
- Nested capture.
