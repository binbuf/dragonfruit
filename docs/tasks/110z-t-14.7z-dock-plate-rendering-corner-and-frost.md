## Goal

The plate reads as one perfect rounded rectangle in every state: the bright rim
follows the corner curve instead of poking past it, the QML plate and the
compositor's frosted panel are the same shape (no 1 px fringe or edge
flicker), and the artwork↔plate padding measures even at rest and mid
magnification.

## Context (read first)

- `shell/dock/Dock.qml` — the plate group (`~2970-3053`):
  `dockBar` (fill, radius `Theme.controls.dock.radius` 28), `dockRim`
  (a 1 px-tall, `rimThickness/2`-radius hairline at `y = plateY`, inset by
  `rimInset = plateRadius * 0.6` ≈ 16.8), and `dockBorder`. Because
  `rimInset < radius`, the rim's ends sit **on top of the corner arcs**: it
  draws a straight bright line ~11 px into each curve — the reported
  "artifacting at the top edges before the curves begin".
- `compositor/src/window/backdrop.rs` — the Dock's frosted panel is drawn by
  the compositor from the declared panel rect: `explicit_panel_bounds`
  (`~300-308`) + `backdrop_elements` (`~327-348`), which decomposes a rounded
  rect into piecewise solid spans with `radius.round()` and
  `to_physical_precise_round(scale)`. That is a second rounded rectangle
  behind/through the translucent QML fill.
- `shell/src/shellcontroller.cpp` `renderDock` (`~5992-5996`) sends the panel
  as `qFloor(x), qFloor(y), qCeil(w), qCeil(h)` while the QML plate draws the
  fractional `plateRect` — up to a 1 px shape mismatch that moves each frame
  (the reported "background height grows/shrinks by tiny bits back and
  forth").
- `design-system/tokens/tokens.json` — `component.dock.radius` 28,
  `plate.rimHeight` 1, `fillOpacity` 0.42, `rimOpacity` 0.7; the T110u gotcha
  says the radius is **static** and shared by the shell plate, the compositor
  Dock backdrop, and the gallery demo (do not make it size-tracking here).
- `docs/PROGRESS.md` T110u measured the resting paddings (artwork top inset
  15 px, dot 4 px with an 8 px gap and a 3 px margin); the resting numbers are
  right — the artifact is the rounded-rect rendering and the live alignment.
- Live report (2026-09-27): *"the background is not a perfect rounded
  rectangle, it has little artifacting at the top edges before the curves
  begin… the padding between the tiles and the background is uneven."*

## Current behavior (reproduced, T110u era)

- A bright line/hairline artifact where the rim meets the corner curves at the
  plate's top edge; the frost edge and the QML fill edge disagree by a pixel
  while the plate grows, so the apparent padding changes frame to frame; at
  rest the padding is even (T110u measurement) but the artifact makes it read
  uneven.

## Scope

- [ ] Make the rim follow the rounded shape: inset it by at least the plate
      radius, or clip a rim layer to the plate's rounded path (a mask / an
      inner `border` on a rounded rect), so no straight segment protrudes into
      a corner arc. Keep `rimHeight`/`rimOpacity` from tokens; restore the
      reference look (a bright top rim that fades into the corners).
- [ ] Align the QML plate and the compositor frost: pick one owner for the
      visible rounded edge, or make the two rects identical (the same integer
      rect and radius). Options: send the panel rect on the same integer grid
      the QML plate snaps to; snap the QML plate to that grid; or drop the
      compositor's solid tint layer for `MaterialRole::Dock` so the frost
      stays behind the QML fill without drawing a second visible edge. Keep
      the one-backdrop-pass invariant and the degrade tiers (ADR 0102).
- [ ] Stabilize the committed panel rect: only call `setDockPanelRect` when
      the integer rect actually changes (building on T-14.7y's damped plate
      top), so a smooth animation cannot ping-pong the compositor panel by a
      pixel.
- [ ] Measure and record, at rest and mid-magnification: top/bottom/end
      artwork insets and the corner radius; assert no 1 px row flips across a
      pointer sweep. Add pixel tests that a bright rim pixel follows the
      corner arc and that no rim pixel lies outside the plate shape.
- [ ] Captures `docs/captures/t14-dock-plate-corners-{light,dark}.png`
      (resting corner close-up) and a magnified strip; update the T110u
      capture notes if the measured values move.
- [ ] Tests: `tst_dock` pixel/geometry cases for the rim and alignment,
      `tst_design_system` gallery unaffected, compositor backdrop unit tests,
      `make check` unchanged.

## Explicitly deferred

- True liquid-glass refraction (ADR 0102).
- A size-tracking plate radius tied to `barThickness` (T-16.1a; T110u gotcha).
- Icon squircle masking (T-14.7w) and folder/Trash artwork internals.
- Per-output chrome sizing (T-16.1a).

## Design notes

- One rounded-rect source of truth. Two independently rasterized rounded
  rects (QML AA vs compositor piecewise spans) will always show a seam; either
  make them the same geometry or make one invisible.
- The radius stays static (T110u gotcha); do not recompute it from the plate
  height.
- Preserve the layered-glass intent (fill, rim, border, shadow) and the
  `Minimal` degrade capsule (ADR 0102) — fix the shape, don't remove layers.
- The plate is cosmetic: no reserved-zone, input-region, or settings change.
