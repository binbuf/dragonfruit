Decisions / gotchas for T-15.8b and later:

- **Reuse is load-bearing.** The adapter depends on `dragonfruit-session` to
  reuse `IdlePolicy`/`IdleStage`; do not re-model the idle chain or re-time a
  stage in the pane. `display_off_after()`/`lock_after()` are derived from that
  policy.
- **The adapter is read-only.** The compositor owns the lock, the session owns
  the timing, and the display preferences are settingsd's. The pane writes
  `lock.*` keys (not yet declared — T-15.8b adds them, likely revision 15) and
  the session/compositor applies them live; do not add a write to the adapter.
- **No live D-Bus source.** Lock state and idle policy are not on the bus; the
  concrete source is the shell's `df_toplevel_manager` client. T-15.8b wires
  the pane/tile (shell-native + settingsd keys, per the T-15.5b Mission Control
  precedent) rather than a system-status host. The mock is the tested path.
- **Linux adaptation:** the session blanks/locks at one delay each, so the
  macOS battery/AC display-off pair collapses to the single `blank` stage.
- **`LockDisplayOption::id()` spellings** are the intended settings key
  suffixes (`showUserNameAndPhoto`, `showPasswordHints`,
  `showMessageWhenLocked`, `showPowerButtons` under a `lock.` prefix).
- Live visual check: `bash scripts/capture-t15-lock-adapter.sh` produced
  `docs/captures/t15-8a-lock-adapter.png` (3840x2160). No surface of its own,
  so the capture confirms only that the nested desktop renders; vision found
  the menu bar, Dock, wallpaper, Settings window, and the X11 demo window
  composited with no blank regions or stray artifacts.
