## Hand-off

**Landed.** The backend serves `org.freedesktop.impl.portal.FileChooser`
(version 3) at `/org/freedesktop/portal/desktop` and advertises it in
`dragonfruit.portal` / `model::BACKEND_INTERFACES`. The test client drives
OpenFile/SaveFile to a normalized path, a cancellation answers code 1, and the
diagnostic `ListDirectory` proves the files-core link. Contract frozen in
[ADR 0076](../../design/adr/0076-filechooser-portal-and-presenter-seam.md).

- `portal/src/chooser.rs` (new) — the pure model. `ChooserKind`
  (`OpenFile`/`SaveFile`/`SaveFiles`), `ChooserOptions` (decodes `multiple`,
  `directory`, `modal`, `accept_label`, `current_name`, `current_folder`,
  `current_file`, `files`; the `ay`/`aay` byte arrays are null-terminated),
  `ChooserRequest` (keeps the raw options too), `ChooserResponse`
  (`success`/`cancelled`/`other`, `uris()`), `normalize_uri` (files-core
  `Location`: a plain path or `file://` URI becomes canonical; any foreign
  scheme is discarded), `ChooserRegistry` (begin/complete/cancel/handles/
  request; duplicate handle refused), the one-shot `ChooserCompletion`/
  `ChooserCompleter` future, and `list_directory`/`DirectoryListing`/
  `ChooserEntry` over `DirectoryModel` + `StdFsSource`.
- `portal/src/interfaces.rs` — `FileChooserPortal` +
  `FILE_CHOOSER_INTERFACE`. `OpenFile`/`SaveFile`/`SaveFiles` build a
  `ChooserRequest`, register it, emit the diagnostic `FileChooserOpened`, and
  `await` the completion; `Version` is 3. The standard method blocks until the
  presenter answers, which is the impl-portal contract.
- `portal/src/dbus.rs` — `Backend` gained the shared `chooser` registry
  (`with_services`, `chooser()`); `initialize` serves `FileChooserPortal`.
  Diagnostic presenter half on `org.dragonfruit.Portal1`:
  `PendingFileChoosers() -> a(ss)`, `CompleteFileChooser(handle, uris) -> b`,
  `CancelFileChooser(handle) -> b`, `ListDirectory(uri) -> a(ssbu)`, and the
  `FileChooserOpened(handle, kind, app_id, parent_window, title, options)`
  signal.
- **Dependency** — `portal` now links `dragonfruit-files-core` (a library, not
  a process) for `Location` and the listing.
- **Tests** — `portal/tests/filechooser.rs` (new, 5 integration on a private
  bus; the client acts as the presenter). Unit tests in `chooser.rs`
  (options/bytes/normalization/registry/completion/listing) and
  `interfaces.rs`. `session_bus.rs` now expects the third interface;
  `portals.rs` introspects it.
- **Docs** — ADR 0076; `07-system-integration.md` "FileChooser (T-13.2a)".

Gotchas for later tasks:

- **T-13.2b owns the picker.** There is no dialog yet: a request stays pending
  until a presenter calls `CompleteFileChooser`/`CancelFileChooser` (or the
  calling client gives up). The T-13.2b shell picker should watch
  `FileChooserOpened`, browse with `ListDirectory` (or its own files-core
  bridge), and complete with local paths or `file://` URIs — the portal
  normalizes either.
- **The `uris` result is the only success payload** (plus `writable=false` for
  open). Filters/choices are not interpreted in T-13.2a; the raw options reach
  the presenter through `FileChooserOpened` and `ChooserRequest::raw_options`,
  and echoing selected `choices`/`current_filter` is T-13.2b.
- **`ListDirectory` is diagnostic and blocks** while files-core lists; it is
  the seam, not the final transport. The picker may list in-process instead.
- `normalize_uri` treats a scheme-less string as a local path; `trash://` and
  every other foreign scheme are discarded.
- The `FileChooserOpened` signal and the presenter methods live on
  `org.dragonfruit.Portal1`, never on the standard interface.
- T-13.2b adds the picker UI.
