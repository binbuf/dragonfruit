## Reference UI/UX (macOS screenshots)

Near-verbatim target for the Keyboard and Trackpad panes: local images in
`docs/reference/macos/` (never shipped; see
[14-risks.md](../design/14-risks.md)), distilled in
[System_Preferences.md](../reference/System_Preferences.md). Our sidebar keeps
separate `Keyboard`, `Mouse`, and `Trackpad` panes per the reference IA (the
track title combines them). The macOS Mouse pane was **not** captured — its
rows are unobserved; flag a follow-up capture.

Sections and rows (observed order):

- **Keyboard** — `Key repeat rate` slider (`Off`/`Slow`/`Fast`), `Delay until
  repeat` slider (`Long`/`Short`), `Adjust keyboard brightness in low light`
  toggle, `Keyboard brightness` slider, `Turn keyboard backlight off after
  inactivity` popup (`Never`), `Press ⌘ key to` popup (`Show Emoji &
  Symbols`), `Keyboard navigation` toggle with helper text and `Keyboard
  Shortcuts...` button; then `Text Input`: `Input Sources` popup (`U.S.`)
  with `Edit...`, then `Text Replacements...`; then `Dictation` (omit, see
  below): toggle, `Languages` popup, `Microphone source` popup.
- **Trackpad** — preview graphic, then a segmented `Point & Click` /
  `Scroll & Zoom` / `More Gestures` tab bar (Point & Click shown). Point &
  Click rows: `Tracking speed` slider (`Slow`/`Fast`), `Click` slider
  (`Light`/`Medium`/`Firm`), `Force Click and haptic feedback` toggle with
  helper text, `Look up & data detectors` popup (`Force Click with One
  Finger`), `Secondary click` popup (`Click or Tap with Two Fingers`), `Tap
  to click` toggle (`Tap with one finger`), `Set Up Bluetooth Trackpad...`
  button. Scroll & Zoom and More Gestures contents were not captured.

Repeat rate/delay map to `input.repeatDelay`/`input.repeatRate` (settingsd →
compositor/input; `docs/design/tracks/08-settingsd-live-settings.md`).
`Tracking speed`, scrolling, and `Tap to click` map to `PointerSettings` in
`compositor/src/input/settings.rs` (no settingsd keys yet; provider TBD
(T-15.4a)). All other rows are the pane's target UI, providers TBD (T-15.4a).

**Adapt or omit (Apple-only):** the `Dictation` section (Siri/Apple
Intelligence), the `⌘` glyph in `Press ⌘ key to` (Linux Super/Meta
substitution — note it, keep the row), and captured Apple device names like
`Automatic (MacBook Pro Microphone)`; `Set Up Bluetooth Trackpad...` stays as
the generic device-setup action.
