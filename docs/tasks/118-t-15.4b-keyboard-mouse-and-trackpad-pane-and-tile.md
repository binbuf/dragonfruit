**Sections and rows (observed order):** Observed in the Tahoe capture(s):
- `Keyboard` (top group): `Key repeat rate` (slider, ~70%, `Off`/`Slow`/`Fast`).
- `Keyboard` (top group): `Delay until repeat` (slider, ~80%, `Long`/`Short`).
- `Keyboard Brightness`: `Adjust keyboard brightness in low light` (toggle, on).
- `Keyboard Brightness`: `Keyboard brightness` (slider, ~60%, dim/bright sun
  glyphs at the ends).
- `Keyboard Brightness`: `Turn keyboard backlight off after inactivity` (popup,
  `Never`).
- `Keyboard Brightness`: `Press ⌘ key to` (popup, `Show Emoji & Symbols`).
- `Keyboard Navigation`: `Keyboard navigation` (toggle, off) — helper text
  "Use keyboard navigation to move focus between controls. Press the Tab key to
  move focus forward and Shift Tab to move focus backward.", with a trailing
  `Keyboard Shortcuts...` button.
- `Text Input`: `Input Sources` (popup, `U.S.`) with a trailing `Edit...`.
- `Text Input`: `Text Replacements...` (button).
- `Dictation`: `Dictation` (toggle, off) with helper text "Use Dictation
  wherever you can type text..."; `Languages` (popup, `English (United
  States)`) with `Edit...`; `Microphone source` (popup, `Automatic (MacBook Pro
  Microphone)`).
- `Trackpad` preview: a simulated trackpad and a gesture-preview panel.
- `Point & Click` / `Scroll & Zoom` / `More Gestures` (segmented tabs, `Point &
  Click` selected).
- `Point & Click`: `Tracking speed` (slider, ~60%, `Slow`/`Fast`).
- `Point & Click`: `Click` (slider, ~70%, `Light`/`Medium`/`Firm`).
- `Point & Click`: `Force Click and haptic feedback` (toggle, on) — helper
  "Click then press firmly for Quick Look, Look up, and variable speed media
  controls.".
- `Point & Click`: `Look up & data detectors` (disclosure row, `Force Click
  with One Finger`, right chevron).
- `Point & Click`: `Secondary click` (disclosure row, `Click or Tap with Two
  Fingers`, right chevron).
- `Point & Click`: `Tap to click` (toggle, on) — helper "Tap with one finger".
- `Trackpad` footer: `Set Up Bluetooth Trackpad...` (button) with a trailing
  `?` help.
- `Mouse`: not captured — its rows are unobserved; flag a follow-up capture.
- Header/chrome: centered pane title, back/forward chevron pill (forward
  dimmed), sidebar `Search` field, trailing `?` help.

**Apple-only (adapt or omit):** the `Dictation` section (Siri/Apple
Intelligence); the `⌘` glyph in `Press ⌘ key to` becomes Dragonfruit's keymap
label (Super/Meta, [keymap.md](../keymap.md)) — keep the row; captured Apple
device names like `Automatic (MacBook Pro Microphone)` become generic; `Force
Click and haptic feedback` is Apple hardware behavior, and our equivalent is
decided later; `Set Up Bluetooth Trackpad...` stays as the generic
device-setup action. Repeat rate/delay map to
`input.repeatDelay`/`input.repeatRate` (settingsd → compositor/input;
`docs/design/tracks/08-settingsd-live-settings.md`); `Tracking speed`,
scrolling, and `Tap to click` map to `PointerSettings` in
`compositor/src/input/settings.rs` (no settingsd keys yet; provider TBD
(T-15.4a)); all other rows are the pane's target UI, providers TBD (T-15.4a).
