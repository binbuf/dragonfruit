## Reusable facts / gotchas

- The capture plan uses `\x1f` as its field separator: pre-capture shell
  commands contain `|`, so a pipe-delimited plan truncates them.
- The live query is taken **after** the capture. This is required for
  `lock-exit`, `fullscreen` and `popover`; a pre-driver query reports the
  stale state.
- The nested output here is 2880x1800 at scale 1/1.5/2. The scorer must use
  the query's **physical** panel rect and scale the logical band offsets, or
  a 1.5x/2x row samples the wallpaper.
- `lock-exit` needs the shell's unlock retry: the lock surface can take
  longer than the requested delay to configure on the nested host.
- The dev demo launches the shell with `QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen`, so
  `DRAGONFRUIT_CHROME_GPU=1` does not produce dmabuf in the T207 capture
  rail. `make t21-gpu-rollout` is the dmabuf evidence.
- Remove each `$SOCKET.session` after a full-session row or the runtime
  tmpfs fills; the T207 runner does this.
- The moving-panel helper is compiled into the scratch dir and exits after
  25 s; the runner kills its pid file if the case ends early.
