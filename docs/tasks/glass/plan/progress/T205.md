# T205 — Notification banner, stack and lower-panel materials

## What landed

- **Three optical presets.** `notificationBanner`, `notificationStack` and
  `notificationLowerPanel` already existed in `tokens.json`; T205 consumes them
  on the live surfaces and labels the uncovered Dark variants:
  - banner Light 0.549/0.976 (measured), Dark 0.56/0.30 (adapted);
  - stack Light 0.551/0.706, Dark 0.507/0.550 (measured);
  - lower Light 0.407/0.852 (measured), Dark 0.40/0.30 (adapted, toned below
    the Dark stack and distinct from the Dark banner).
- **Compositor flat-band family.** `compositor/src/render.rs` adds the three
  notification presets to the replacement flat-band branch, so the sampled
  pass consumes the resolved alpha/tint directly instead of the generic Popup
  adaptive tint. `compositor/tests/material_presets.rs` pins the values,
  distinctness and Dark adaptation.
- **Banner region.** `NotificationBanner.qml` resolves `notificationBanner`
  and declares one `banner` region sized to the visible card (96 pt, or 132 pt
  with actions), not the surface's transparent strip. The QML card is
  transparent on the sampled path and paints the resolved fill on
  solid/software/Reduce Transparency. The QML shadow is disabled on the
  sampled path so it cannot darken the opaque compositor flat band.
- **Center regions.** `NotificationCenter.qml` resolves `notificationStack`
  and `notificationLowerPanel` and declares two disjoint regions, `stack` and
  `lower`, separated by the panel gap. The stack holds the title and scrollable
  history; the lower panel is a fixed summary/hint footer. The empty state is
  deliberate (`No notifications` / `Notifications unavailable`) and the
  overflowing history shows a visible thumb plus `Scroll for more`.
- **Co-visibility.** `ShellController::applyNotificationCenterData()` sets
  `bannerInset` from the active banner card height (96/132 + 8 pt), so the
  stack starts below the banner and the two trusted surfaces never overlap or
  sample each other.
- **Protocol/shell plumbing.** `ShellProtocol` gains
  `setBannerPanelRegions`/`clearBannerPanelRegions` and
  `setNotificationCenterPanelRegions`/`clearNotificationCenterPanelRegions`
  (shared `applyPanelRegions` helper, now also used by Control Center). The
  shell commit-latches the lists with each image, re-renders on
  `materialRegionsChanged`, and clears them before unmapping.
- **Capture fixture.** `MockNotificationClient` accepts
  `DF_NOTIFY_FIXTURE=empty|populated|concurrent|scrollable|banner-empty`; the
  shell opens the Center with `DF_NOTIFY_CENTER_FIXTURE=open`.
  `t21-live-capture.sh` gained a `notification-center` state and
  `FORCE_MATERIAL_PATH=gpu|software`.
- **Evidence.** `scripts/t205-notification-materials.py` (check/scorer/report)
  and `scripts/capture-t205-notification-materials.sh`; `make
  notification-materials-check` is in `make gates`, and `make
  notification-materials-capture` runs 18 nested cases.
- **Docs.** ADR 0224, `docs/design/10-design-system.md`,
  `docs/design/02-compositor.md`, `docs/design/04-shell.md`,
  `docs/private-protocols.md`.

## Region / preset mapping

| Surface | Region key | Preset | Extent |
|---|---|---|---|
| `notification` | `banner` | `notificationBanner` | visible card, 0..96 or 0..132, radius 14 |
| `notification-center` | `stack` | `notificationStack` | y=`bannerInset`..lower-gap, radius 14 |
| `notification-center` | `lower` | `notificationLowerPanel` | y=`height-64`..height, radius 14 |

The stack and lower rects are disjoint; the banner is a separate surface. A
banner-empty capture shows all three materials with the Center's empty state.

## Measurements / evidence

`docs/captures/t205-notification-materials.json` / `.md`, 18 cases:

- banner neutral sampled fit error 0.0006–0.0014; grid 0.0364–0.0372;
- stack neutral 0.0008–0.0019; grid 0.0003–0.0019;
- lower neutral 0.0013–0.0016; grid 0.0026–0.0029;
- Reduce Transparency / software up to 0.0141 (opaque plate);
- all grid regions attenuate the backdrop variance; stack/lower/banner rects
  never overlap; region count is 2 (Center) + 1 (banner), well under 32.

Dark banner and Dark lower-panel rows are marked `underconstrained: true` and
carry `confidence: adapted`; the Dark stack is the measured 0.507/0.550 fit.
The nested host rendered 2880x1800, so the scorer uses the live query rects
rather than assuming 1920x1200.

## Reusable facts / gotchas

- The compositor flat-band shader output is **opaque**. Do not stack a second
  alpha over it; use the resolved own-layer value directly. This is why the
  banner and Center are inset instead of overlapping.
- On the sampled path the QML plate must be transparent **and its QML shadow
  hidden**. A QML shadow under a transparent fill shows through and darkens
  the measured material (banner error was ~0.115 before the fix). The shadow
  returns on solid/software/Reduce Transparency.
- Sample blank material strips, not row cards/text: banner right padding,
  stack right padding outside the rows, lower panel top band. Row cards are
  opaque `surfaceMuted`/`surfaceElevated` and otherwise dominate the mean.
- `materialRegions` is a normal QML property; the shell connects its generated
  `materialRegionsChanged` signal. Re-render on it because `mapToItem`/layout
  changes do not necessarily damage the producer.
- `DF_NOTIFY_FIXTURE=concurrent` puts an active banner with Reply/Open actions
  plus three history rows in the Center; `banner-empty` is the all-three-
  materials empty case; `scrollable` has 18 rows.
- The nested capture output can be 2880x1800 even with
  `DRAGONFRUIT_NESTED_SIZE=1920x1200`; parse `query scale` region rects.
- Remove each `$SOCKET.session` after a case or the runtime tmpfs fills; the
  T205 capture script does this.

## Follow-ups

- Dark banner/lower panel remain underconstrained. Recapture a controlled
  Dark notification surface over black/mid/white and replace the adapted
  0.56/0.30 and 0.40/0.30 values if the reference gains independent fits.
- The lower panel is currently a summary/hint footer, not a widget/calendar
  panel. If a future task adds widgets, keep the `lower` region key and swap
  the content; do not add a second overlapping material.
- The banner/center shadow is QML-only on solid/software paths and absent on
  the sampled path. If the compositor grows a measured notification shadow,
  move it there and remove the QML shadow entirely.
- Keyboard Tab traversal still reaches only the list rows (T204 follow-up);
  action buttons are pointer targets. A future accessibility pass can add
  action-level focus traversal.
