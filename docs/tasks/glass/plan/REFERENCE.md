Research basis is `macos-liquid-glass@063a473cf06d0dba5cb83bd56c40b6fc88df2931` and Dragonfruit `94129f74b5ef` on `release/v0.1.1`. The workstation records one macOS 27.0.1 build (26A434), with 3600×2338 capture pixels representing 1800×1169 logical points at 2×. Use logical points for geometry and optical widths and convert once for the target output. Preserve embedded ICC profiles and convert to sRGB before scoring. Keep source pixels and target outputs separate from the palette or colour space used internally by the GPU.

## How to resolve conflicting evidence

- The three slider anchor samples win over the rounded linear formulas. At 0.5255 the reported formula predicts α ~0.565 and tint ~0.976. Piecewise interpolation preserves the measured 0.552/0.993 point. Use the measured File curve for light attached menus and label application to other light popover presets as Dragonfruit policy. Dark slider response is unmeasured. Hold Dock, window, and system-panel slider extremes at their baseline presets until separately calibrated.
- Fit Dock magnification from the measured layout sweep and use 73/60 for the default reference fixture. Multiplying diagnostic amplitude by 73/128, as suggested in the prose model, gives ~1.61×, inconsistent with the stated 1.217×. Preserve independent configurable size and peak rather than scaling absolute widths blindly.
- Reject the published Dark File α/tint 0.136/0.598 as a calibration target. The fit crop `(205, 200, 225, 1200)` in `captures/coverage/dark_menu_file__neutral_white.png` is pixel-identical to the Light File crop in `captures/raw/neutral_white__menu_file.png`. After ICC conversion, the reported crop's R means are 0.9861, 0.4337 and 0.1220 for white/mid/black. Use the confirmed Dark mid/black captures as appearance checks and document the attached-menu baseline as an adaptation pending a controlled recapture. The Dark context/status regressions also have sizable residuals (RMSE ~0.064 Desktop, ~0.068 Wi-Fi). Use separate presets and compare against their raw sample outputs.
- Coverage JSON has automated bounding boxes that span the whole frame or disagree with the visible panel. Verify geometry against the PNG and AX dump where available. Treat Control Center and Notification Center nested-region fits as observed effective output. Stacking each fitted alpha independently would compound the result. Use the later controlled Control Center fit for the outer baseline, and validate Wi-Fi expanded and Bluetooth list independently. Document discrepancies between the two campaigns and any light context crop whose tint fit exceeds the sRGB range.
- Reference `analysis/out/validation.json` scores a NumPy approximation. The flat band p95 is ~0.071 even for neutral-mid, with SSIM ~0.896, and checker_16 p95 ~0.162. These miss the proposed gates. The reference `compare.py` resizes the candidate and compares a rectangle. Add bounded registration, ICC conversion and material-only masks to score the actual Dragonfruit output.
- Direct local spot check of `captures/raw/neutral_black__dock_rest.png` at x=1800 yielded sRGB levels 115, 112, 104, 91, 62, 15, 2, 0 at y=10/30/50/70/100/150/180/190 px after ICC conversion, matching the black veil LUT. The sampled top-band crop is identical for the two raw Dock slider endpoints. Neither check establishes behavior on other macOS builds or displays.

## Integration constraints

- Reuse `chrome_blur_panels()`, `MaterialRegion`, stable panel keys and the existing scene/blur caches. The shared code is in `compositor/src/{render.rs,window/}`, with GLES paths in nested and DRM. Keep renderer effects separate from the SHM/dmabuf chrome transport and presentation scheduling. Preserve normal z-order, lock isolation, fullscreen suppression, direct scanout, and output-specific damage.
- Separate optical presets from the existing four `MaterialRole` tier/fallback classifications. Use generated `design-system/tokens/tokens.json` data and the existing token generator. QML owns content and an explicit fallback when compositor material is unavailable. Assign tint, rim and shadow once. For reduce transparency, skip sampling and lensing and use a scheme-appropriate accessible plate. Increase Contrast raises edge/foreground contrast, including in the combined override. Reduce Motion governs timing independently.
- Keep backdrop reads within the compositor. The sibling demo's `ShaderEffectSource` samples a same-scene wallpaper, its QML motion uses different easing, and its KWin integration is a skeleton. Use the measurements and test methods with Dragonfruit's private `df_shell` path, same-output scene and trusted-role checks. The research checkout is provenance, with no build dependency on its scripts, generated demo shaders or captures.
- Measurements for dark veil, dark notification banner/lower panel, interactive auto-hide, multi-output, fractional DPI, HDR/P3 and several secondary surfaces are incomplete or absent. Name Dragonfruit defaults as adaptations. Preserve existing Launchpad-equivalent Apps drawer, OSD, Mission Control, switcher, lock, and service behavior. Gate broader appearance work on surface-specific evidence.

## Evidence convention

Capture through trusted compositor output using deterministic wallpapers and a separate moving client behind the chrome. Record revision, output/scale/refresh, backend, material preset/tier/fallback and settings, device/driver, capture method and source hash. Scope material scores to matched logical crops excluding text, icons and other overlays. Measure rim, lens, MTF and timing separately. Record native and nested runs separately. Capture missing Mac cases when hardware is available, including controlled post-login slider extremes with settings restored afterward. Missing evidence stays explicit in the report.
