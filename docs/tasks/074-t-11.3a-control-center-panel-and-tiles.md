## Reference UI/UX (macOS screenshots)

Near-verbatim target: the real macOS quick-settings sheets — local images in
`docs/reference/macos/` (never shipped; see
[14-risks.md](../design/14-risks.md)); distilled in
[macos-ui-inventory.md](../reference/macos-ui-inventory.md). Only the Wi-Fi
and Bluetooth sheets were captured (`Screenshot 2026-09-18 at 9.23.53 PM.png`,
`Screenshot 2026-09-18 at 9.23.47 PM.png`); the full Control Center panel and
the Focus/dark-mode tiles were not captured — these two sheets are the only
tile references.

Sheet anatomy (both captures): modal sheet, rounded, no title bar; bold title
top-left with an on/off toggle aligned top-right; flat rows separated by thin
dividers; circular icon tiles; a trailing `... Settings...` text link; the
Bluetooth sheet adds frosted-glass vibrancy.

- **Wi-Fi** — `Wi-Fi` toggle (on); `Known Network`: `<network>`, connected
  (blue icon) and secured (lock icon); `Other Networks` (expanded): network
  rows with gray icons and lock icons; `Other...` and `Wi-Fi Settings...`.
- **Bluetooth** — `Bluetooth` toggle (on); connected device rows
  (`WF-1000XM6`, `WH-1000XM6`) with headphone icon tiles, informational only;
  `Bluetooth Settings...`.

The Wi-Fi tile maps to the NetworkManager adapter (T-07) and the Bluetooth
tile to the BlueZ adapter (T-15.1a); each `... Settings...` link opens the
matching pane, and the panel keeps the keyboard shortcut and Escape/click-away
dismissal.
