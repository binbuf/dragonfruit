- `docs/captures/t17-visual-floor.md` (reviewed sign-off),

  `docs/captures/README.md`, `docs/design/10-design-system.md`, `Makefile`.

### Sign-off

- **Materials, light and dark:** present and consistent with the `window_*`/
  `ssd_*` goldens; live tones `chrome=2d2534ff` (dark) / `chrome=ffffffff`
  (light). `check-gallery-snapshots.py --strict` is 84/84.
- **Reduced motion passes:** `reduced_motion_sweep` 3/3 (20 motion tokens, 57
  QML sites, 6 lifecycle kinds), the `*_dark_reduced` goldens under `--strict`,
  and the runtime conformance suites in `make e2e`. The live reduced-motion
  still is pixel-identical to the dark still.
- **"No flat approximations" — waived with a reason.** No untokened flat fill
  remains; the backdrop is the token-driven feather approximation of ADR
  0013/0091/0122 (no GPU sampler), which ADR 0122 already names as a future
  material-track item. The true sampler is a recorded follow-up.

### Deviations / follow-ups

- The live window menu would not open on this host's X11/Qt SSD clients
  (right-click on the titlebar left `query window-menu` closed); the popup
  material is covered by the design-system `menu_*`/`popup_*` goldens. Recorded
  in the capture notes, not silently omitted.
- The nested iGPU budget drives the material ladder to `reduced` (`degrade
  tier=reduced`); the `full` tier still rendered 35 frames. T-17.4 owns the
  performance verdict.

## Live visual check (required)

Before reporting done, run the app and **look at it**:

- Launch the nested session (`make demo`), open the surface this task changed,
  and capture it (e.g. `spectacle -b -n -f -o /tmp/<task>.png`).
- Inspect the image: all UI/UX must be present and read as intended in the
  compositor window — layout, materials, text, and no stray artifacts.
- A green `make e2e` is necessary but **not sufficient**: the headless suites
  render no pixels, so they miss exactly this class of bug. If the task has no
  surface of its own, still launch the demo and confirm the desktop renders.
- Note the capture path and your verdict in the Hand-off.
