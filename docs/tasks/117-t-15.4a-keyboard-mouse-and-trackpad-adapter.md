## Hand-off

**Landed.** A new workspace crate `dragonfruit-input` (`services/input`) is the
libinput keyboard/mouse/trackpad adapter. It implements the T-07 contract
(`InputAdapter` + `AdapterId::INPUT`) behind an `InputSource` seam with a
`MockInput`, and is deliberately **read-only** (libinput persists nothing and
has no setter; the user's settings stay settingsd-owned and compositor-applied,
per design principle 3 / FR-4).

**Decisions for T-15.4b.**

- The adapter is the **device inventory** (which keyboard/mouse/trackpad, their
  capabilities and libinput built-in defaults), not a settings owner. `Tracking
  speed` / `Tap to click` / scrolling are **settingsd keys consumed by the
  compositor** (add them with the pane, as T-15.3b added the `sound.*` keys);
  `input.repeatDelay`/`input.repeatRate` already exist.
- The snapshot classifies by capability (`DeviceKind`); a pointer with
  gesture/tapping is a `Touchpad`, any other pointer a `Mouse`. `device_changes`
  is the pure event diff.
- Absence has two hide rules: libinput gone/inaccessible → `Unavailable`; a
  running stack with no device → `Available` + `present() == false`.
- T-15.4b follows the `*Host`/`*Client` precedent (Bluetooth/Storage/Sound) if
  it projects the inventory through the bridge host; `AdapterId::INPUT` and the
  view are ready for that.
- Live tool output is human-oriented and may change; the fixture is the
  tripwire and the parser ignores unknown keys.
