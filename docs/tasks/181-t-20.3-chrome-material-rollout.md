# T181 — T-20.3 Chrome material rollout

## Goal
The sampled blur + liquid-glass material reaches **every** chrome surface, not
just the menu bar and drawer: the Dock, context menus, menu-bar dropdowns,
popovers, the OSD, notification banners, and Control Center. The shell's QML
"glass claim" layers become a token-driven complement to the compositor material
rather than standing in for it.

## Context (read first)
- `docs/design/adr/0182-tahoe-liquid-glass-material-pass.md` · `docs/design/tracks/20-tahoe-liquid-glass.md`.
- T179/T180 — the pass and the liquid-glass fields.
- `compositor/src/window/backdrop.rs` — `MaterialRole::from_layer_namespace`;
  add/verify the role for each surface.
- `compositor/src/render.rs` — `is_backdrop_panel` and the panel-bounds logic;
  overlays with no explicit output, popovers, etc.
- `compositor/src/shell/layer.rs` — `panel_rect`; the drawer already declares it
  (T179); the Dock already does.
- `shell/dock/Dock.qml`, `shell/menubar/*`, `shell/control-center/*`,
  `shell/notifications/*`, `shell/osd/*` — the QML surfaces/layers that should
  now lean on the compositor material.
- `design-system/tokens/tokens.json` — per-role material tokens.

## Scope
- [ ] Verify/complete `MaterialRole` selection and `is_backdrop_panel` for every
      chrome layer: menu bar (`Chrome`), Dock (`Dock`), menus/context menus/
      popovers/OSD/notification/Control Center (`Popup`), and the drawer
      (`Drawer`, via `set_panel_rect`).

- [ ] Tune per-role material tokens so each surface reads correctly light/dark
      (`material.chrome*`, `dock*`, `popup*`, drawer), regenerating the theme.
- [ ] Drop the QML surfaces' redundant opaque fill so the compositor material
      shows through; keep their token-driven rim/hairline as the complement.
      `check-design-tokens` stays green.
- [ ] Rollout captures for the Dock, a context menu, a menu-bar dropdown, the
      OSD, a notification, Control Center, and the drawer; light and dark.

## Out of scope
- New material maths (done in T179/T180).
- Multi-GPU and the software fallback (→ T182).
- Non-chrome surfaces (windows, the lock screen) — out of the material track.

## Design notes
- The rollout is per-surface **configuration**, not new machinery: the pass and
  the role resolution already exist. Any surface that is not frosted is a bug in
  its role/panel rect, not a missing pass.
- Keep the one-pass guard: rollout must not add a second backdrop request per
  frame (`backdrop_skipped` stays zero).
- Control Center/notifications are `overlay` surfaces with their own radius; the
  component radius token drives the backdrop corner, so confirm
  `component.<surface>.radius` matches the QML drawn radius.
- Menu-bar dropdowns are `Popup` but persist while open; confirm the backdrop
  follows the open menu's rect (the popup overlay surface).
