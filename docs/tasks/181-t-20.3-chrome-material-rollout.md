# T181 — T-20.3 Chrome material rollout

## Goal
The sampled blur + liquid-glass material reaches **every** chrome surface, not
just the menu bar and drawer: the Dock, context menus, menu-bar dropdowns,
popovers, the OSD, notification banners, and Control Center. The shell's QML
"glass claim" layers become a token-driven complement to the compositor material
rather than standing in for it.

## Context (read first)
- `docs/design/adr/0182-tahoe-liquid-glass-material-pass.md` · `docs/design/tracks/20-tahoe-liquid-glass.md`.
- T179/T180 — the pass and the liquid-glass fields.
- `compositor/src/window/backdrop.rs` — `MaterialRole::from_layer_namespace`;
  add/verify the role for each surface.
- `compositor/src/render.rs` — `is_backdrop_panel` and the panel-bounds logic;
  overlays with no explicit output, popovers, etc.
- `compositor/src/shell/layer.rs` — `panel_rect`; the drawer already declares it
  (T179); the Dock already does.
- `shell/dock/Dock.qml`, `shell/menubar/*`, `shell/control-center/*`,
  `shell/notifications/*`, `shell/osd/*` — the QML surfaces/layers that should
  now lean on the compositor material.
- `design-system/tokens/tokens.json` — per-role material tokens.

## Out of scope
- New material maths (done in T179/T180).
- Multi-GPU and the software fallback (→ T182).
- Non-chrome surfaces (windows, the lock screen) — out of the material track.

## Design notes
- The rollout is per-surface **configuration**, not new machinery: the pass and
  the role resolution already exist. Any surface that is not frosted is a bug in
  its role/panel rect, not a missing pass.
- Keep the one-pass guard: rollout must not add a second backdrop request per
  frame (`backdrop_skipped` stays zero).
- Control Center/notifications are `overlay` surfaces with their own radius; the
  component radius token drives the backdrop corner, so confirm
  `component.<surface>.radius` matches the QML drawn radius.
- Menu-bar dropdowns are `Popup` but persist while open; confirm the backdrop
  follows the open menu's rect (the popup overlay surface).

## Hand-off

**State: done.** The sampled blur + liquid-glass material now reaches every
chrome surface, and the QML side is a token-driven scrim rather than the
material.

### Changes

- **Compositor role/rect (configuration, no new pass).**
  `compositor/src/window/backdrop.rs` adds `MaterialRole::corner_radius(namespace)`
  and `spec_for`/`blur_spec_for`, and `compositor/src/render.rs` resolves each
  panel through them. Role selection (`from_layer_namespace`) is unchanged and
  now covered for every namespace: menu bar `Chrome`, Dock `Dock`, drawer
  `Drawer`, and menu-bar/dock popups, OSD, notifications, Control Center
  `Popup`. `is_backdrop_panel` keeps full-output scene overlays (Mission
  Control/switcher) flat. The OSD rounds from `component.osd.radius` (20 px);
  every other popup from `component.popup.radius` (14 px), matching the QML
  cards.
- **Shell panel rect.** New `ShellProtocol::setOsdPanelRect` (mirrors the
  Dock/drawer `set_panel_rect`); `Osd.qml` reports its visible card
  (`panelRect`) and `renderOsd` declares it, so the frost covers the 180×180
  card and not the 220×220 surface's transparent shadow margin.
- **QML scrim.** `design-system/components/{Popup,Popover,ContextMenu,
  MenuBarMenu}.qml`, `shell/control-center/ControlCenter.qml`,
  `shell/osd/Osd.qml`, `shell/notifications/NotificationBanner.qml`, and the
  Dock popovers (`DockWindowChooser`, `DockStackPopover`, `DockOverflowPopover`,
  `DockTrashEmptyPopover`, `DockAppPicker`) put `material.popupOpacity` in the
  fill (alpha in the *fill*, not item opacity) and keep the `border` hairline
  opaque.
- **Tokens.** `design-system/tokens/tokens.json` `material.popupOpacity`
  0.96/0.92 → **0.62/0.70** light/dark; `Theme.qml` + `design_tokens.rs`
  regenerated.
- **Capture.** `DF_MENUBAR_MENU_FIXTURE=<index>` seam in `shellcontroller.cpp`
  opens the fixed system dropdown (the status row's x drifts with the clock, so
  a synthetic click is not stable). `scripts/capture-t20-chrome-material.sh` +
  `scripts/t20-chrome-material-driver.py` (new) and 14 committed stills
  `docs/captures/t20-chrome-{dock,menubar,contextmenu,controlcenter,osd,notification,drawer}-{light,dark}.png`.
- **Docs.** New `docs/design/adr/0183-chrome-material-rollout.md`;
  `docs/design/02-compositor.md` (rollout paragraph), `docs/design/04-shell.md`

### Deviations / gotchas

- The menu-bar **status** menus (Wi-Fi/volume/battery) are inert under
  `DF_STATUS_FIXTURE`: the fixture views omit `"visible"`, so
  `applyStatusMenuData` marks those slots `available: false` and
  `openStatusMenu` is never reached. The committed menu-bar dropdown is the
  fixed system menu, opened through the new fixture seam. A synthetic click on
  the bar also does not open the app/system menus (only the Control Center
  item did); the capture seam sidesteps that.
- The OSD fixture auto-dismisses after 1.4 s, so `capture_osd` polls the demo
  log for the one-shot `OSD scene-graph commit path active` marker and shoots
  immediately.
- Control Center pointer interaction (slider drag) does not register through
  the synthetic harness in this session, so the OSD is presented by the
  fixture rather than the CC slider.
