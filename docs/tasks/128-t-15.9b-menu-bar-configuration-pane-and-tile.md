**Sections and rows (observed order):** Observed in the Tahoe capture:
- First untitled behavior group:
- `Automatically hide and show the menu bar` (popup, `In Full Screen Only`).
- `Show menu bar background` (toggle, off).
- `Recent documents, applications, and servers` (stepper field, `10`).
- `Menu Bar Controls` header: "System and app controls can be configured to
  appear in both Control Center and the menu bar." with an `Add Controls...`
  button.
- `Clock` (list row without a checkbox, trailing `Clock Options...` button).
- `Spotlight` (checkbox, checked).
- `Wi-Fi` (checkbox, checked).
- `Bluetooth` (checkbox, checked).
- `Battery` (checkbox, checked, trailing `Battery Options...` button).
- `AirDrop` (checkbox, checked) — Apple-only.
- `Focus` (checkbox, checked, popup `Show When Active`).
- `Screen Mirroring` (checkbox, checked, popup `Show When Active`).
- `Display` (checkbox, checked, popup `Show When Active`).
- `Sound` (checkbox, checked, popup `Show When Active`).
- Header/chrome: centered pane title, back/forward chevron pill (forward dimmed), sidebar `Search` field, trailing `?` help.

**Apple-only (adapt or omit):**
- AirDrop and Screen Mirroring (Continuity/AirPlay) have no Linux equivalent.
- Sidebar Apple Account, Apple Intelligence & Siri, and Screen Time rows.
- Spotlight keeps its row as our Search entry (naming substitution only).
- The sidebar user row `Daniel Gillespie` / `Apple Account` (capture shows the
  real account name; generic `<user>` for us).
