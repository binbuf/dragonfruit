**Sections and rows (observed order):** Observed in the Tahoe capture:

- `Privacy` header — blue hand-with-shield icon, title `Privacy`, and the
  description "Control which apps can access your data, location, camera, and
  microphone, and manage safety protections."; `Learn more...` link.
- `Location Services` — disclosure row, blue location-pin icon, badge `1`,
  trailing chevron.
- `Calendars` — disclosure row, secondary `1 full access`.
- `Contacts` — disclosure row, secondary `None`.
- `Files & Folders` — disclosure row, secondary `18 apps`; FileChooser portal
  (T-13.2a/b).
- `Full Disk Access` — disclosure row, secondary `1 full access`.
- `Home` — disclosure row, secondary `None`.
- `Media & Apple Music` — disclosure row, secondary `3 apps`.
- `Passkeys Access for Web Browsers` — disclosure row, secondary `1 full
  access`.
- `Photos` — disclosure row, secondary `3 full access`.
- `Reminders` — disclosure row, secondary `None`.
- `Accessibility` — disclosure row, badge `2`, trailing chevron.
- `App Management` — disclosure row, badge `3` (partially visible).
- Header/chrome: centered pane title, back/forward chevron pill (forward dimmed), sidebar `Search` field, trailing `?` help.

The badge counts (`1`, `2`, `3`) and secondary values (`18 apps`,
`3 full access`, …) are the capture's example data, not fixed values.

**Apple-only (adapt or omit):** Apple Advertising; Analytics sharing to Apple;
Find My; FileVault; Lockdown Mode (capture ends before the Security section).
`Media & Apple Music` keeps a media-library access row and drops the Apple
Music half; `Passkeys Access for Web Browsers` maps to the host Secret Service
(reuse-only).

## Hand-off

**Landed (T-15.13b, done):** the Settings `Privacy & Security` pane and the
Control Center `Privacy` tile as one unit over the T-15.13a portal
PermissionStore adapter, through `org.dragonfruit.SystemStatus1.Privacy` in
`services/system-status` (ADR 0143). New: `services/system-status/src/privacy.rs`
(+ `tests/privacy.rs`), `apps/settings/PrivacyClient.{h,cpp}`,
`apps/settings/PrivacyPane.qml`, the `privacy` painted glyph in
`design-system/components/Icon.qml`, and the shell read-only path
(`systemstatusclient`/`systemstatusmodel`/`shellcontroller`/`ControlCenter.qml`
16th tile + internal-gap compaction). Tests: `tst_settings_privacy.{cpp,qml}`,
`tst_settings_absence.qml` / `tst_settings_shell.qml` (shipped 19 → 20),
`tst_controlcenter.qml` (16 tiles), `tst_statusmodel.cpp`.

**Deviations / decisions:** no settingsd key — the store is the state
(contrast the Printers pane's paper size). The macOS rows are replaced by the
14 portal tables; no Calendars/Photos/Full Disk Access/etc. rows are invented.
Allow/Deny toggles become a four-way `Allowed`/`Denied`/`Ask`/`Not Set`
selector so `ask` and removal are reachable. The panel now carries 16 tiles:
the tiles' internal gap is compacted `sm` → `xs`; a 17th tile would need more
compaction or a taller nested output (the capture scripts assume 1920×1200 with
a 1160 panel; no pointer-axis forwarding, so no scroll).

**Verification:** `cargo test -p dragonfruit-system-status -p
dragonfruit-privacy-adapter` green; `ctest --test-dir build -j4` 67/67;
`make check-design-tokens check-tokens check-no-capture-grab` clean; gallery 78
green. Live visual check: `scripts/capture-t15-privacy-pane.sh` →
`docs/captures/t15-13b-privacy-pane.png` and
`docs/captures/t15-13b-privacy-control-center.png`; vision found no clipping or
artifacts and the intended header, category rows, and tile.

**Next (T-15.14a Accessibility adapter):** follow the same bridge-host pattern;
T136 blocks it, so this hand-off is the immediate predecessor.
