**Sections and rows (observed order):** Observed in the Tahoe capture:

- `Privacy` header — blue hand-with-shield icon, title `Privacy`, and the
  description "Control which apps can access your data, location, camera, and
  microphone, and manage safety protections."; `Learn more...` link.
- `Location Services` — disclosure row, blue location-pin icon, badge `1`,
  trailing chevron.
- `Calendars` — disclosure row, secondary `1 full access`.
- `Contacts` — disclosure row, secondary `None`.
- `Files & Folders` — disclosure row, secondary `18 apps`; FileChooser portal
  (T-13.2a/b).
- `Full Disk Access` — disclosure row, secondary `1 full access`.
- `Home` — disclosure row, secondary `None`.
- `Media & Apple Music` — disclosure row, secondary `3 apps`.
- `Passkeys Access for Web Browsers` — disclosure row, secondary `1 full
  access`.
- `Photos` — disclosure row, secondary `3 full access`.
- `Reminders` — disclosure row, secondary `None`.
- `Accessibility` — disclosure row, badge `2`, trailing chevron.
- `App Management` — disclosure row, badge `3` (partially visible).
- Header/chrome: centered pane title, back/forward chevron pill (forward dimmed), sidebar `Search` field, trailing `?` help.

The badge counts (`1`, `2`, `3`) and secondary values (`18 apps`,
`3 full access`, …) are the capture's example data, not fixed values.

**Apple-only (adapt or omit):** Apple Advertising; Analytics sharing to Apple;
Find My; FileVault; Lockdown Mode (capture ends before the Security section).
`Media & Apple Music` keeps a media-library access row and drops the Apple
Music half; `Passkeys Access for Web Browsers` maps to the host Secret Service
(reuse-only).
