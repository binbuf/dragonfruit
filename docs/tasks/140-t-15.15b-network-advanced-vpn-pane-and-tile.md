## Hand-off

**State: done.** The Settings `Network` pane (`NetworkPane.qml`) and the
Control Center `VPN` tile ship as one functional unit over the T-15.15a adapter,
through the bridge host (ADR 0147), not by linking the Rust crate. The adapter
owns the connection state, so the pane has **no new settingsd key**: its two
writes are the adapter's `Connect`/`Deactivate`, by UUID. Wi-Fi has its own
`wifi` pane; Firewall and Apple-only services are omitted (ADR 0122) rather
than shipped as dead rows.

Decisions / gotchas for T-15.16 and later:

- **No settingsd key.** NetworkManager owns the connection state; the pane's
  writes are the adapter's two, by UUID. Do not add a durable VPN preference.
- **Absence is layered (ADR 0146/0147).** `unavailable` = no system bus / no
  `NetworkManager` owner; `available` + `present: false` = the daemon answered
  with no VPN (pane empty note, tile hides). `readOnly: true` is a polkit
  degradation, not absence: the list stays visible and the writes disable.
- **The Control Center panel surfaced a real integration bug.** The vpn tile
  first captured blank because `ShellController`'s startup refresh block did
  not call `refreshVpn()`; the headless model/QML tests inject the `vpn`
  property directly, so they cannot catch a missing controller refresh. Fixed
  and re-captured.
- **The panel is 1164 px and its Accessibility/VPN tiles are single-line.**
  Eighteen tiles fit only because the compact tiles are single-line (icon
  `tileSize: 24`) and `kControlCenterHeight` is 1164 (the nested output's max
  below the bar). A 19th full tile would need another compaction.
- Live visual check: `bash scripts/capture-t15-vpn-pane.sh` produced
  `docs/captures/t15-15b-vpn-pane.png` (2088×1410) and
  `docs/captures/t15-15b-vpn-control-center.png` (360×1164). Vision read the
  Settings `Network` pane (`VPN` group: `Work VPN` connected, `Home` idle, live
  toggles, no clipping/overlap) and the panel's `VPN` tile (`Work VPN`,
  second-from-bottom above Clipboard, fully visible, no artifacts).
- Not touched (other tasks): launching Settings on a pane from the Control
  Center (`onVpnSettingsRequested` is the log-only T-16 stub), the Firewall
  pane, and the menu-bar VPN status item.
