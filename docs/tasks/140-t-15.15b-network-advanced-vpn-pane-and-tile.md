## Reference UI/UX (macOS screenshots)

Near-verbatim target: the macOS Network pane (`Screenshot 2026-09-18 at
9.21.03 PM.png`) — local images in `docs/reference/macos/` (never shipped; see
[14-risks.md](../design/14-risks.md)); distilled in

**Sections and rows (observed order):**

- **Wi-Fi** — status row: icon, label, green dot with secondary `Connected`,
  trailing chevron pushing the Wi-Fi pane; provider TBD (T-15.x).
- **Firewall** — status row: icon, label, gray dot with secondary `Inactive`,
  trailing chevron; provider TBD (T-15.x).
- **`Other Services`** — section header over service rows
  `USB 10/100/1000 LAN` and `Thunderbolt Bridge`, each `Not connected` (red
  dot) with a trailing chevron; providers TBD (T-15.x).
- **Footer** — `...` dropdown and `?` help button.
- **VPN and advanced network settings** — no VPN row and no sub-pane are
  visible in the capture, so their nesting is unconfirmed; map VPN rows and
  the connections behind the chevrons to NetworkManager over the adapter's
  D-Bus seam (T-15.15a, never `libnm`).
- **Sidebar and IA** — one continuous list: Search field, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth,
  `Network` selected (blue), then the other system rows; the macOS
  Wi-Fi/Network panes have no tracked task yet, and this capture is the only
  reference for that IA.

**Adapt or omit (Apple-only):** iCloud Private Relay and Apple-specific
services (none appear in this capture); the Apple Account, Apple Intelligence
& Siri, and Screen Time sidebar rows.
