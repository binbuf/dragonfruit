## Reference UI/UX (macOS screenshots)

Near-verbatim target: the real macOS quick-settings sheets — local images in
`docs/reference/macos/` (never shipped; see
[14-risks.md](../design/14-risks.md)); distilled in
[macos-ui-inventory.md](../reference/macos-ui-inventory.md). Only the Wi-Fi
and Bluetooth sheets were captured (`Screenshot 2026-09-18 at 9.23.53 PM.png`,
`Screenshot 2026-09-18 at 9.23.47 PM.png`); the full Control Center panel and
the Focus/dark-mode tiles were not captured — these two sheets are the only
tile references.

The same sheet anatomy applies to the Focus and dark-mode tiles, but there is
no direct capture of either tile: modal sheet, rounded, no title bar; bold
title top-left with an on/off toggle aligned top-right; flat rows separated by
thin dividers; circular icon tiles; a trailing `... Settings...` text link
into the matching pane. The Focus and dark-mode toggles keep the same
accessible roles and Escape/click-away dismissal as the captured sheets.
