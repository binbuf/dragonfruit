## Goal

Every Dock click does exactly what the macOS-like click tree promises, and a
launch never silently no-ops. Today clicking a pinned app may not open a window
and clicking a running app may not switch to it; this unit audits the whole
path, fixes the causes, and makes a failure visible instead of silent.

## Current behavior / hypotheses to confirm (T101 era)

- **Launch environment.** The shell forces `QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen` for
  itself and `appLaunchEnvironment` switches it to `wayland` for children, but
  children still need the session's `WAYLAND_DISPLAY`/`XDG_RUNTIME_DIR` (or
  `DISPLAY`). If the shell was launched with `--socket-name` and did not
  export `WAYLAND_DISPLAY`, a launched client has no display and never maps —
  which presents as "clicking does nothing".
- **Activation has no failure signal.** `activate_app` is fire-and-forget; if
  the app's `app_id` in the projection does not match the window's app id, or
  the window is gone, the Dock does nothing and the user sees a dead click.
- **Tap/drag arbitration.** A click that drifts past the DragHandler threshold
  becomes a rearrangement instead of an activation; a click while the pointer
  arrived through the transparent band, or while a bounce runs, should still
  activate.
- **Missing identity.** After T-14.7 an absent app-index leaves pinned
  entries unresolved (`missing`); clicking must say so (notice), not no-op.

## Scope

- [ ] Reproduce the full matrix **nested and (if available) on a real
      session**, with logging at each hop (QML signal → `onDockEntryActivated`
      → launch/activate → compositor), and record which cases fail before
      fixing. Matrix: pinned not running; pinned running one window; pinned
      running multiple windows (chooser); minimized entry; temporary running;
      recent; missing/unresolved; stack/folder; Trash; divider.
- [ ] Fix the launch environment so a child always receives the display the
      compositor serves: export `WAYLAND_DISPLAY` (derived from the shell's
      socket name when `--socket-name` is used) and `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR`, keep
      `QT_QPA_PLATFORM=wayland` for the Wayland session, and do not leak
      `offscreen`. Unit-test `appLaunchEnvironment` for the nested and
      DRM/session shapes.
- [ ] Make activation observable: give the shell a way to know activation
      failed (e.g. an additive activation-result event or an ack with a
      found/not-found flag) and fall back to launch or a notice; never leave a
      dead click. Until the compositor event lands, gate on the projection's
      `running`/`windows` and treat an empty app id as a launch.
- [ ] Harden tap/drag arbitration: a stationary click always activates
      (magnified or not, pointer entered via the band or not); a slop-drag
      lifts; document the thresholds. Verify the root dismiss `TapHandler`
      never eats an entry tap.
- [ ] Confirm cross-Space behavior: clicking a running app on another Space
      switches Space and focuses (the `activate_window_id` path), including a
      minimized window restore, and that the window chooser selection does the
      same.
- [ ] Tests: extend `tst_dock.qml` for the tap cases and the
      magnified/band-entry click; `tst_dockcore` for the identity fallback
      (`planAppOpen` + projection); keep
      `dock_click_tree_activation_conformance` green and add a

- [ ] Capture: `docs/captures/t14-dock-activation.png` (before/after a

## Design notes

- The click tree is the product contract from `04-shell.md`; a case that
  cannot work must produce a notice, not silence.
- Keep the compositor as the sole activation authority; the shell never
  re-derives "which window".
- The launch environment must be identical for every launch path (Dock,
  menu bar `openApp`, Files reveal) — one helper, one test.
