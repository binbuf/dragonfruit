## Hand-off

T-13.4b fills the stream. Landed here:

- **Portal**: `portal/src/screencast.rs` (pure model: `SourceType`,
  `ScreenCastOptions`, `ScreenCastSelection`, `ScreenCastStream`,
  `ScreenCastRegistry` with CreateSession/begin_select/complete/cancel/start,
  one-shot completion), served as `org.freedesktop.impl.portal.ScreenCast`
  version 3 with `CreateSession`/`SelectSources`/`Start` +
  `AvailableSourceTypes`/`AvailableCursorModes` in `portal/src/interfaces.rs`
  (`ScreenCastPortal`, `ScreenCastSessionObject`). `portal/src/dbus.rs` gains
  the shared registry, the diagnostic `ScreenCastOpened` signal, and
  `CompleteScreenCast`/`CancelScreenCast`/`PendingScreenCasts`. The
  `dragonfruit.portal` descriptor and `model::BACKEND_INTERFACES` list it.
  `portal/tests/screencast.rs` proves the whole lifecycle on a private bus.

- **Shell**: `shell/src/screencastbridge.{h,cpp}` (dockcore) watches
  `ScreenCastOpened`, publishes the source list and selection, and answers the
  portal; `shell/screenshot/ScreenCastPicker.qml` is the pure design-system
  picker (Screens/Windows sections, checkbox/radio selection, Cancel/Share).
  `ShellProtocol` gains the centred `screencast` overlay surface and
  `screencastSources()` (monitors from outputs, windows from toplevels);
  `ShellController` filters by `types`, maps/renders the surface, and presents a
  deterministic fixture under `DF_SCREENCAST_FIXTURE`. Tests:
  `shell/tests/tst_screencast.cpp` (bridge + fake portal round-trip) and
  `tst_screencastui.{cpp,qml}` (the view).

- The contract is frozen in ADR 0080.

**T-13.4b must know:**

- The backend advertises ScreenCast **version 3**. Persistence
  (`persist_mode`/`restore_data`, v4) and virtual monitors are *not*
  advertised; bump the version and add a restore-data format when streaming
  supports them.
- `Start` currently returns each stream with `node_id = 0` and a Dragonfruit
  `id` property carrying the chosen source handle (plus `source_type`). The
  chosen handle format the shell produces is `monitor:<name>` and
  `window:<toplevel-windowId>`; T-13.4b maps it to a PipeWire node and may add
  `size`/`position`/`pipewire-serial`.
- The source-type bitmask is `1` monitor, `2` window; `cursor_mode` defaults
  to `1` (hidden) and the shell carries it only in the raw options.
- The picker surface is created at startup and unmapped until a request; a
  missing portal is a silent, supported state.

## Live visual check

`DF_SCREENCAST_FIXTURE=1 make demo DEMO_ARGS="--socket-name dragonfruit-t94"`
rendered the picker over the desktop; full-screen still
`/tmp/opencode/t94/screencast.png` captured with `spectacle -b -n -f` and
analysed with `.symphony/symphony vision`. Verdict: centred card titled "Share
your screen" with the requesting app's subtitle, sections "Screens" and
"Windows", rows "Built-in Display" (1920 × 1080), "Settings", and "Files" with
their ids and empty selection boxes, the "Choose one or more sources" hint, and
the Cancel / Share footer; the dialog is on top of the X11 demo window and
fully unobstructed, no clipping. The log confirms `ScreenCast scene-graph
commit path active`.
