## Goal

Fade/scale a closing window out while it stays input-inert, removing it from the model only at completion.

## Scope

- Fade/scale out; window input-inert and removed from the model only at completion.

## Explicitly deferred

- Interrupt/reverse/retarget and the idle trace (T-02.4b).

## Acceptance (agent-completable)

- [ ] A closing window fades out and is removed exactly once; it takes no input.

## Test plan

- Headless: close transition commits removal exactly once.

## Hand-off

**Landed.** `WindowMotionKind::Close` (reverse of appear/restore, timed on
`WINDOW_CLOSE`) plus `is_ghost() = Minimize | Close`. `DfState::close_window`
unmaps the window immediately, starts the close ghost from the app's Dock tile
(or centered fallback), and sends the client close request. The model entry is
removed exactly once when the motion settles, via the new single teardown path
`DfState::remove_window`, which broadcasts the new `WindowEventKind::Closed`.
`toplevel_destroyed`/`destroy_x11_window` defer while `WindowModel::is_closing`
so a client destroy mid-ghost cannot double-remove. `render.rs` ghost walks use
`is_ghost()` and skip dead windows; `apply_workspace_layout` never remaps a
closing window. New synthetic hook `close <window-id>`; `query motion` reports
`close`, `query events` reports `closed`.

**Tests.** `window_conformance.rs::close_fades_out_inert_and_commits_removal_exactly_once`
(21/21) asserts the tile origin/target, that the window stays tracked while the
ghost is live, that a click over its old rect reaches no client (input-inert),
and that removal is broadcast exactly once. `make cargo-test`, `make clippy`,
`make fmt-check`, `make e2e` all green.

**Deviations.** None from the plan. Per the acceptance ("close transition
commits removal exactly once") removal is committed by the transition, not by
the client destroy; a client veto of the advisory `xdg_toplevel.close` is not
modelled yet — noted for T-02.4b.

**Docs.** ADR `0007-close-ghost-deferred-removal.md`;
`docs/design/02-compositor.md` "Window lifecycle motion".

**For T-02.4b.** Add interruption/retarget and the idle trace. Keep the single
`remove_window` path and the `is_closing` deferral; do not register a second
motion driver.
