**Sections and rows (observed order):** Observed in the Tahoe capture:
- Header card `Notifications`: red bell icon, title `Notifications`, and the
  description "Customize when and how notifications appear, if they play a
  sound, and which apps can send them." plus a `Learn more...` link.
- `Notification Center`: description "Notification Center shows your
  notifications in the top-right corner of your screen. You can show and hide
  Notification Center by clicking the clock in the menu bar."
- `Notification Center`: `Show previews` (popup, `When Unlocked`).
- `Notification Center`: `Show Notifications:` sub-row label.
- `Notification Center`: `when display is sleeping` (toggle, off).
- `Notification Center`: `when screen is locked` (toggle, on).
- `Notification Center`: `when mirroring or sharing the display` (popup,
  `Notifications Off`).
- `Application Notifications`: per-app disclosure rows — icon, name, state
  subtext, and a right chevron. Captured rows: `Antigravity IDE` (`Off`),
  `Calendar` (`Off`), `Creative Cloud` (`Badges, Sounds`), `Cryptomator`
  (`Off`), `Discord` (`Badges, Sounds`), `FaceTime` (status cut off in the
  capture, not readable).
- `Focus`: a sibling sidebar pane sharing the same service state; its own
  capture is not in the set.
- Header/chrome: centered pane title, back/forward chevron pill (forward dimmed), sidebar `Search` field, trailing `?` help.

**Apple-only (adapt or omit):** the `Learn more...` link in the header
description; anything Apple-account-related; sidebar `Apple Account`,
`Apple Intelligence & Siri`, and `Screen Time` rows.
