## Reference UI/UX (macOS screenshots)

Local art-direction only — images in `docs/reference/macos/` (never shipped;
see [14-risks.md](../design/14-risks.md)), distilled in
[System_Preferences.md](../reference/System_Preferences.md). The capture shows
the Notifications pane selected, with **Focus** a sibling sidebar entry whose
own pane is not captured. Target: mirror the panes nearly verbatim, minus the
Apple-only bits below.

Sections and rows (observed order):

- **Header card**: title "Notifications", red bell icon, one-line description.
- **Notification Center**: `Show previews` popup (`When Unlocked`); a
  `Show Notifications:` subheader; then `when display is sleeping` toggle
  (off), `when screen is locked` toggle (on), and `when mirroring or sharing
  the display` popup (`Notifications Off`).
- **Application Notifications**: per-app rows — icon, name, short state
  subtext (`Off`, `Badges, Sounds`), trailing chevron; a row opens per-app
  settings (that screen is not in the capture).
- **Focus**: sibling pane backed by the same service state.

Mapping: our notification service owns global policy and the app list; the
visibility conditions and app rows are its policy surface. Focus/DND is the
one source of truth for the pane, its tile, and the menu bar
([04-shell.md](../design/04-shell.md),
[08-settings.md](../design/08-settings.md)).

Adapt or omit (Apple-only): the `Learn more...` link in the header
description; anything Apple-account-related.
