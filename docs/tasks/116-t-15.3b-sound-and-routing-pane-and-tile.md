## Reference UI/UX (macOS screenshots)

Local art direction only: real macOS screenshots in `docs/reference/macos/`
(never shipped; see [14-risks.md](../design/14-risks.md)), distilled in
[System_Preferences.md](../reference/System_Preferences.md). The macOS Sound
pane is our near-verbatim target — same grouped inset sections, order,
labels, and control types.

Sections and rows (observed order):

- `Output`/`Input` tabs over the `Name`/`Type` device table, one row
  selected (captured: `MacBook Pro Speakers` / `Built-in`); the pane opens
  on `Output`; `Input` is unobserved in the capture.
- `Sound Effects`: `Alert sound` popup with a preview play button,
  `Play sound effects through` popup, `Alert volume` slider, then three
  toggles: `Play sound on startup`, `Play user interface sound effects`,
  `Play feedback when volume is changed`.
- `Output volume` slider with `Mute` checkbox, then `Balance` slider
  (`Left`/`Right`).

Map output/input device selection, `Output volume`, and `Mute` to the
PipeWire/WirePlumber adapter (T-15.3a); keep `Sound Effects` and `Balance`
as the pane's target rows, with providers TBD (T-15.x).

Adapt or omit (Apple-only): nothing strongly Apple-only here; alert-sound
names (captured: `Boop`) are ours to choose.
