**Sections and rows (observed order):** Observed in the Tahoe capture(s):
- `Sound Effects`: `Alert sound` (popup, `Boop`) — with a trailing play button
  to preview.
- `Sound Effects`: `Play sound effects through` (popup, `Selected Sound Output
  Device`).
- `Sound Effects`: `Alert volume` (slider, ~80%) — low/high speaker icons at
  the ends.
- `Sound Effects`: `Play sound on startup` (toggle, on).
- `Sound Effects`: `Play user interface sound effects` (toggle, on).
- `Sound Effects`: `Play feedback when volume is changed` (toggle, off).
- `Output & Input`: `Output` (selected) / `Input` (segmented) — the pane opens
  on `Output`; `Input` is unobserved.
- `Output & Input`: device table columns `Name` / `Type`; one row selected
  (captured `MacBook Pro Speakers` / `Built-in`).
- `Output & Input`: `Output volume` (slider, ~30%, low/high speaker icons) with
  a `Mute` (checkbox, checked) control.
- `Output & Input`: `Balance` (slider, centered ~50%, `Left`/`Right`).
- Header/chrome: centered pane title, back/forward chevron pill (forward dimmed
  at the end of history; this capture shows both enabled), sidebar `Search`
  field, trailing `?` help.

**Apple-only (adapt or omit):** Nothing strongly Apple-only in the rows
themselves; the captured alert-sound name `Boop` is ours to choose, and the
captured device name `MacBook Pro Speakers` becomes a generic output-device
name. Map output/input device selection, `Output volume`, and `Mute` to the
PipeWire/WirePlumber adapter (T-15.3a); keep `Sound Effects` and `Balance` as
the pane's target rows, with providers TBD (T-15.x).

## Hand-off

**State: done.** The Sound pane and Control Center tile ship together over the
T-15.3a audio adapter. Routing (`SetDefaultSink`/`SetDefaultSource`), `Output
volume`, and `Mute` round-trip through the bridge host; `Sound Effects` and
`Balance` are settingsd keys (new `sound` group, schema revision 11) so no
control is dead. Absence is documented and tested. See
`docs/design/07-system-integration.md` "The Sound pane and tile (T-15.3b)" and
ADR `docs/design/adr/0123-sound-pane-and-tile.md`.

Deviations: the reference's alert-sound preview button and `?` help are omitted
(no alert playback engine yet; ADR 0122), and per-application stream routing /
input volume stay out of scope. The pane is taller than the window and scrolls;
the capture shows its top half.

Tests: `cargo test -p dragonfruit-system-status` (25+9+4), `cargo test -p
dragonfruit-settingsd` (33 + schema_doc), `ctest` 56/56, `make e2e` EXIT 0.
Live check: `scripts/capture-t15-sound-pane.sh` →
`docs/captures/t15-3b-sound-pane.png` / `-control-center.png`; vision verified
both surfaces render with no clipping in the panel.

Next: T-15.4a (Keyboard, Mouse, and Trackpad adapter). The status bridge's
`Audio` interface now has 2 extra methods; follow the same `*Host`/`*Client`
precedent for the new adapters.
