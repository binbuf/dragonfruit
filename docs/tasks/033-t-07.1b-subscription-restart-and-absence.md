## Goal

Event subscription with re-subscribe on service restart and explicit absence handling.

## Scope

- Event subscription (no polling above the adapter); re-subscribe on service restart.
- Absent daemon means hidden or an explicit unavailable state, never an error and never a startup blocker.

## Explicitly deferred

- Concrete adapters (T-07.2 through T-07.4).

## Acceptance (agent-completable)

- [ ] Restarting the mock re-subscribes; absence hides the slot.

## Test plan

- Headless: kill/restart mock; assert re-subscribe and absence.
