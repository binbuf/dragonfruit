## Hand-off

**Landed.** The live `ColorScheme` is compositor state on `DfState`
(`set_color_scheme`, default dark) and every compositor-drawn material — SSD
titlebar, window menu, chrome backdrop, window shadow — resolves its tokens
from it. `query material` and `set color-scheme light|dark` expose it over the
synthetic harness; `window_conformance::
material_schemes_select_and_reduced_motion_stays_single_frame` proves both
schemes and reduced motion headless. ADR 0016 and `02-compositor.md`
"Light/dark and reduced motion (T-04.4b)" are the contract. Captures:
`docs/captures/t04-materials*` from `scripts/capture-materials.sh`, including
`t04-materials-gallery-side-by-side.png` against the `ssd_light`/`ssd_dark`
goldens and `t04-materials-before-after*.png` (degrade `minimal` blur-off vs
full); `check-gallery-snapshots.py --strict` is 66/66 green.

**Deviations.** The pre-material baseline uses the T-04.4a `minimal` degrade
tier (blur off) rather than a true pre-T-04 build; it is the same material with
the named "blur off" quality floor, not a git-reverted renderer. The QML shell
chrome does not follow the compositor scheme (the shell is not notified), so
the light/dark difference is visible on the compositor SSD titlebar/menu/shadow
and the desktop background, not on the Dock — T-08 owns wiring one scheme to
both sides.

**For T-05.** Consume the pass for live surfaces; keep reading
`DfState::color_scheme` (one scheme field, no second source). T-08 mirrors
`appearance.colorScheme` into `set_color_scheme`. The nested capture driver
detects the output under dark and then applies the requested scheme because the
desktop background is scheme-dependent — keep that ordering.
