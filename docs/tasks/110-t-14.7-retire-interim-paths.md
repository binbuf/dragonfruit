## Hand-off

Status: done.

What landed:

- The Dock's local `.desktop` resolver is gone:
  `DesktopEntryIndex::scan`, `parse`, `defaultApplicationDirs`, and the
  absent-service `m_index.scan()` fallback in `ShellController::start` are
  deleted. The cache is populated only from
  `org.dragonfruit.AppIndex1::Enumerate` (T-14.1a), so identity and themed
  icons have one owner. `DesktopEntry` and the launcher
  (`buildLaunchCommand`/`appLaunchEnvironment`) stay in
  `shell/src/desktopentry.{h,cpp}`.
- The `demoAppMenu()` stand-in is deleted. `shell/src/menubrokerclient.{h,cpp}`
  is the real client: `applyFocusedApp` pushes `SetFocusedApp` +
  `SetWindowStates` (`menubrokerpolicy::windowStatesJson`) and renders
  `ResolveFocused`'s `applicationMenuItems` + `menus`. With the service absent
  the shell keeps `fixedApplicationMenu` (no exported menus).
- `tools/dragonfruit-dev` starts `dragonfruit-app-index` and
  `dragonfruit-menu-broker` best-effort (session-bus only) before the shell, in
  both `dev --shell` and `make demo`.

Deviations / what next tasks must know:

- The shell does not yet route a clicked bridged `dbusmenu:<id>` row back to
  app-index's `WindowMenuEvent` (still logs); that is the T-14.4 follow-up.
- Settings still does not call `Publish`; the first-party native tier stays
  `none` until it does. The broker transport is live, so publishing is the only
  remaining step.
- The app-index subscription (T-14.1c) is still unconsumed: the shell loads the
  corpus once at startup (a 500 ms service settle in the dev harness covers the
  race).
- T-15 builds on the real identity; app-index is now the only `.desktop`
  parser.
