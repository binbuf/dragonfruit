## What changed

- Persistent `Chrome`/`Dock` roles stay at `Reduced` with the sampled pass on
  when the global controller selects `Minimal`; the Dock no longer swaps to
  the milky floor-missed plate under transient load (ADR 0211).
- Every downgrade latches its boundary; recovery needs EMA < 0.4 x budget for
  10 s. A per-tier cost guard prevents promoting back into a tier whose last
  EMA was above 1.05 x budget while the current tier is above 0.5 x budget.
- Panel blur chains are cached by scene generation/commit, panel source, blur
  spec, chain size, and scale. A rebuild advances the panel commit so a
  changed blur damages the panel even when its geometry did not move.
- `material stats` adds `blur_skips`.
