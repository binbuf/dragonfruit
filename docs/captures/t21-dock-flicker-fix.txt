## Root cause

`BackdropBlurElement` draws a rounded panel as horizontal spans. Smithay's
damage tracker passes damage in **element-local** coordinates, but
`render_texture_from_to` expects damage local to the **span's destination
rectangle**. The central span starts one corner-radius below the panel origin.
Forwarding panel-local damage unchanged shifted the scissor by that radius (and
the corner spans by their x inset). A small hover/cursor damage rectangle then
missed the real damaged pixels, so the blur panel was not painted there and the
translucent Dock composited over the raw scene underneath.

Fix: `damage_in_span` translates `panel.loc - span.loc`, clips to the span, and
`BackdropBlurElement::draw` passes the result per span. The pure test covers the
central-span offset, out-of-span clipping, and full-panel damage (ADR 0212).
