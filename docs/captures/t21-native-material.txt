## Evidence boundary

- Native: all `material stats`, `degrade stats`, `presentation stats`,
  `chrome stats`, `frame timing`, and `scanout stats` lines above. They come
  from the DRM compositor on DP-1.
- Native visual proof: **not** produced by this task. There is no KMS
  readback or trusted native still here; T191b owns the visual sweep and the
  light/dark/live-content evidence.
- Nested reference: the T190d/T189 captures and stills are reference
  evidence only. They are not native proof.
- Diagnostics-only claims: `path=gpu`, `blur_draws>0`, `blur_panels>=1`,
  `backdrop_skipped=0`, `tier=full|reduced`, refresh/budget, and the chrome
  counters. They do not by themselves prove the blur samples live content.
