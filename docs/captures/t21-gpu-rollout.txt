## Measured exceptions and limits
- The compositor advertises only implicit dma-buf sync (no
  `linux-drm-syncobj-v1`). A shared scene (bar + popup, Dock + popover)
  can fill its three-slot producer ring before the compositor releases
  the previous frame; that frame is a counted `ring-exhausted` shm
  fallback and retries on release. On this shared host the same path can
  also record `sync-timeout`; neither is a black surface or a silent
  copy. A successful dmabuf frame records `copy_bytes=0` (see the clean
  menubar and lock dumps above).
- The private layer protocol and surface roles are unchanged. The
  default remains `DRAGONFRUIT_CHROME_GPU` unset/0; T192/T193 own the
  default-on decision and explicit-sync acceptance.
